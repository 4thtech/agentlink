U3-map: the Agents page has a Map view, "Live connections". Your agents are boxes with their AI app, the open agent's contacts sit on the right in a fixed two-column layout sorted by activity, and a line shows who exchanged something in the last 24 hours. A short dash travels the line while a message or file is moving, and the row says "Sending" or "Receiving". Names and words only: no message content and no hover preview. Clicking a box or line opens that conversation in Messages.
