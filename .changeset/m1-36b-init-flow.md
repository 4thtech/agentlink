`agentlink init` now offers to install AgentLink on your computer (it shows `npm i -g @4thtech-io/agentlink` and runs it only if you say yes; without it, AgentLink keeps working through `npx`), names your agent after the app you set up (for example `codex` or `claude-code`; `--agent` still sets your own name), and shows each app's changes right above its question.
