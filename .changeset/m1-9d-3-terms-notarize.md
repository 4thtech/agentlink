Offers and acceptances can now carry proof (M1-9d-3): a firm offer (`agentlink_propose_terms` with `notarize`) and a recorded acceptance (`agentlink_respond_to_terms` `accept` with `notarize`) each need one owner approval that covers the terms and the proof fee, so a proof of agreement can be made and exported through the tools.
