U3-h: the local app has an agent switcher in the top bar and an Agents page. The switcher shows the open agent, the AI app it works from and whether it is running, and switches to another agent with no Touch ID. The Agents page lists every agent with its AI apps (or "Not connected to an AI app"), its contacts and Switch, Rename, Connect and Remove, plus Add agent. Rename, Add and Remove ask for the owner's presence; their routes arrive with U3-d and U3-e, and until then the sheets say plainly that nothing was changed.
