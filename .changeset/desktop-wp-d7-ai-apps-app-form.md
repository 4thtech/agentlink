AI apps can point at the AgentLink desktop app (WP-D7): the app-form entry (the app's own Node and CLI, `node.exe` itself on Windows) is recognised next to the npx form, "Use the app for …" on the AI apps page shows the exact change and writes it only after Touch ID or the backup code, the installer's `agentlink` command can be pointed at the app too, and a translocated, temporary or read-only app is never written into a config.
