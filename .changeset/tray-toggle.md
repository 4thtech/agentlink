TRAY-TOGGLE: two new switches in Settings › Appearance, kept by the app in ui-settings: "Show AgentLink in the menu bar" (Mac) or "near the clock" (Windows), shown only in the desktop app, and "Show message previews". Both are on unless turned off; any other value is refused.
