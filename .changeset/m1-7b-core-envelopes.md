Add envelopes to the core (`client.envelopes`). Sending builds the envelope after `assertCanSend`: the payload passes its strict schema, the core picks the recipients itself (the contact's agent and owner, the sender's owner and agent, one entry per distinct key; only the agent and its owner on the owner channel), wraps one content key to each of them, signs the envelope with the agent key, checks the 24 KB limit and stores it as sent, with the decrypted payload only in an encrypted column. Receiving checks the structure and the signature and stores the envelope as received with its seen rows. Processing then decrypts it with exactly one key opening and checks the key commitment, the payload, the sender's role (contact, earlier key, unknown sender, owner keys), the owner's entry and the other entries it can recompute, the clock and the envelope ID. Card updates are processed first; an envelope from a contact's new key that arrives before its card update is held for up to 48 hours (at most 100 per owner key) and checked again once the update is applied. Refusals that show a sender signed entries carrying different keys flag the contact. Errors and reports never contain inbound content.
