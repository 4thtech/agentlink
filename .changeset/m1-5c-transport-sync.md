Add subscribe and sync (07 §4.2, §3.2). `SyncEngine.sync({relays, identities, onEnvelope, budgetMs})` sends `REQ {kinds: [1059], "#p": own keys, since}` to each relay, with `since` = the relay's saved cursor minus 48 h and 300 s (a relay with no cursor: 9 days back), opens each gift wrap and hands the envelope to the caller once however many relays serve it. The cursor is saved only after the relay's end-of-stored-events marker and every event of the pass was handled; a failed or slow relay, or an item the handler returns as `retry`, keeps its cursor. Dedup is by gift-wrap id and a wrap is marked seen only with its processed outcome; an envelope id is recorded only when the handler reports `verified`, and is given to the handler as a hint (`envelopeSeen`), never used to skip an item (an unverified envelope's id is chosen by its sender). The default budget is 3 s and the result is flagged `partial` when any relay did not finish. `SyncEngine.subscribe` keeps the subscription open and reconnects with a 5 s to 5 min backoff. Frame counts, queued events, newly opened wraps per pass and the in-memory dedup caches are bounded; relay text and event content never reach a log or a result. `seenPruneBefore` gives the seen-set pruning bound (7 days, or the oldest `since` a sync can still ask for). The cursor and seen-set are an injected `SyncPersistence`. `RelayPool` gains `connect` (a pinned link that is not idle-closed), `onRelayClose` and `isClosed`.
