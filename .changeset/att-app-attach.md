ATT-APP-ATTACH: in the app's conversation the owner can attach light text files (Markdown first; .md, .markdown, .txt, .json, up to 8 MB and 5 files) with Attach or drag-and-drop, see them as chips, remove them, add a note and send them to that person. The files go into the ID's allowed Send folder (never overwriting, never through a link) and travel through the ordinary encrypted send with their own type. Without a Send folder the app shows the Allow your Send folder banner.
