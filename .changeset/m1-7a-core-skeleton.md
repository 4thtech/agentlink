Add the core skeleton: `AgentLinkClient.create` over a KeyStore, a Store, a store cipher and an approval channel. The `Store` interface covers every table of the local store schema (07 §3, with the G-9 delivery, idempotency, owner-authority and budget tables and the staged `pairings`). Its transactions are synchronous and all-or-nothing, updates return counts for compare-and-set, and the store enforces the schema rules itself. An in-memory Store is included for tests. The `ApprovalChannel` interface only delivers requests: nothing it returns approves anything without an owner signature. Identity issues the agent card signed by the owner and endorsed by the agent key, records the agent ID with a random storage prefix, and rotates to the next key epoch with a new card. Until a rotation is complete it refuses to send. Contacts are keyed by agent ID. A pairing is staged from a verified card and committed only when its handshake completes. Contacts follow the re-pair, revocation and key rules and take card updates. A contact can be blocked, or removed as a tombstone, and either way its later envelopes are dropped. Cards, names and relays are stored in columns marked for encryption at rest, sealed through a store-cipher interface; the real cipher (AES-256-GCM under the store key) lands with M1-8b, so nothing is encrypted at rest yet. Display names are marked self-declared.
