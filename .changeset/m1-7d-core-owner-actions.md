Add owner-signed actions and the policy engine to the core. `client.ownerActions.apply` verifies a `SignedOwnerAction` (strict JSON text and schema, the agent and owner binding against the own card, the owner-action signature, the clock skew, expiry and maximum lifetime per op, the nonce) and applies `approval.decide`, `approval.decideBatch`, `approval.cancel` and `policy.set` with the nonce check, its record and the effect in one store transaction, so a replay or a second delivery applies nothing. Decisions are checked against a digest recomputed from the stored request; a cancel of a pending request denies it; a batch applies whole or not at all, never includes a commitment, cannot outlive its earliest request, and burns its nonce when refused. `policy.set` must be above both the stored version and the policy-version mark, which a new `policyMark` option supplies. `loadPolicy` re-verifies the stored policy on every load and fails closed (never the defaults) once a version was applied; `checkApproval` re-verifies a stored decision when its approval is used. The pure `evaluate(policy, action, context)` covers the contacts, envelope, commitment, key-disclosure and receipt rules for M1a. Applying `agent.revoke` and `contact.check` is not part of this change.
