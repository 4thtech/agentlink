Add `contact.check` verify and store (M1-7c-b, 04 §2.5.1, §3.6.1, G-28). `contacts.applyContactCheck(signed)` applies an owner-signed `contact.check` only when it is in its exact strict form, bound to this agent, signed by the owner key in the agent's own card, fresh (`issuedAt` at most 300 s ahead, not expired, lifetime at most 24 h), names a contact with a completed pairing, and its `pairingDigest` equals the digest recomputed from that pairing's stored signed `handshake` and `handshake-ack`. The nonce check, the nonce record (`owner_action_nonces`) and the write of the signed object to `contacts.safety_check` are one store transaction, which also checks that the contact still points at the same pair. Anything else returns `{status: 'refused', reason}` with a fixed code and writes nothing. `ContactView.safetyCodeChecked` (and the `safety-code-checked` label) is now derived on every read by re-verifying the stored object against a fresh recomputation; the `safety_code_checked` column is never read, and a completed re-pair clears it. The pure checks (`checkContactCheckObject`, `verifyContactCheck`, `storedContactCheckHolds`) are exported. `pairing.ownerSafetyCode` now shares the stored-pair recomputation with the contact view.
