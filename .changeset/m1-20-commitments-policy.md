Add the commitments policy to the core (M1-20, D-31, D-55). `evaluateCommitment(policy, facts)` is the pure `commitments` rule for one commitment scope and its stored terms: `deny` blocks, `allow` allows, the default `approval` asks the owner, and a fail-closed policy always asks. Auto-accept applies only to an `accept` or `confirm` from an allowlisted contact whose terms have a price in the USD set (USD, USDC, USDT, XDAI), in the owner's direction and within `autoAcceptMaxUSD` (buy) or from `autoAcceptMinUSD` (sell, off by default); never to a `REJECTED` receipt, a firm offer or terms without a price. A late accept of one of this agent's own superseded versions (`supersededByOwnNewer`) always asks, even under `allow`. A scope and terms that do not bind each other (schema, parties, version or `termsHash`) are `void` with `APPROVAL_VOID` under every policy, and no request is created. An `allow` returns a policy grant with the verified policy's hash; `recheckCommitmentGrant` asks the owner once the policy changed. Also `checkCommitmentBinding`, `commitmentParts`, `commitmentVoidedByPolicy`, `policyDocumentHash` and `isCommitmentAction`. Breaking: the `commitment` policy action now carries `facts`. A batch approval now also refuses an `accepted`/`rejected` acknowledgement, which is a commitment without a scope (D-68).
