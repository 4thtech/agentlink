Add the relay pool, publish and outbox delivery (07 §4.2, §4.3). `RelayPool.publish(event, relays)` sends one signed gift wrap to exactly the relays it is given and nowhere else, all attempts in parallel, and reports a fixed reason per relay (`sent` once 2 relays return OK); relay-sent text never reaches a result, a log or an error. Every connect and reconnect resolves the host and refuses the relay unless every answer is globally routable (IPv4 and IPv6 special-purpose blocks, 6to4, Teredo, NAT64, mapped and CGNAT addresses), then hands the connector the checked address, which the NIP-11 fetch uses too. Inbound frames above 131 072 bytes are dropped before they are parsed. NIP-11 `max_message_length` is read, bounded and cached, and a relay that cannot carry the wrap is skipped. NIP-42 AUTH is signed through `EnvelopeKeys.signNostr`, only when a relay asks for it. At most 64 connections, idle ones closed after 5 minutes, a relay that does not connect within 10 s skipped for that attempt. `OutboxWorker` republishes the stored wrap of each `outbox` row (never re-wrapping) with backoff of 30 s doubling to 1 h, marks it `sent` at 2 OKs and `not_delivered` after 24 h, and reads and writes rows through an injected `OutboxPersistence`. Sockets, DNS and NIP-11 are injected interfaces (`RelayConnector`, `HostResolver`, `Nip11Fetcher`), implemented by `@agentlink/node` and the clients. New dependencies on `@agentlink/types` and `@agentlink/log`.
