W4-HOME: the local app's Home at `/`, simple and Under the hood. The approval card sits on top (+N more, Approve and Deny with Touch ID, and in Under the hood each request's ID, kind and exact expiry and a bulk approve where anything that costs money or commits you stays single). Then Invite someone and People with their last activity. A person opens in place to the conversation both ways (Sent, Waiting to send, Not delivered, Copy, earlier messages), their files (received and sent, Save to Inbox, Show in Finder / Explorer / folder) and a menu with Rename, Check safety code and Remove. Under the hood shows People as a table (handled by, last message, delivery, proofs, files) with search and an agent filter, and each message's ID, exact time, size, agent, receipt and proof.
