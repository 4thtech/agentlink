'@agentlink/crypto': minor

Add the `KeyStore` and `OwnerKeyStore` interfaces (types in the main entry, so transport and core can name them): no method returns a private key or a seed, envelope-hash signing and decryption epochs are included (G-2), and owner signatures exist only on a short-lived session from `unlock(presence)`, which expires and is wiped on lock. Add NIP-44 v2 through `@noble` with strict size, version, base64, MAC and padding checks, Nostr event ids and verification, and a NIP-59 gift wrap whose ephemeral key never leaves the package. `MemoryKeyStore` and `MemoryOwnerKeyStore` (not for production) are exported only from the test-only subpath `@agentlink/crypto/testing`, and the NIP-44 functions that take a secret or conversation key only from `@agentlink/crypto/keystore`. Owner sessions expire on a monotonic clock, `lock()` cancels an unlock in flight, and the owner-channel operations fail closed pending G-2. EIP-712 signing and HPKE opening are declared on the interfaces and fail closed until their tasks land.
