RET-6 + RET-7: Settings › Storage › Keep automatically. Choose how long this computer keeps messages (forever by default) and saved files (new IDs: 90 days; earlier installs: forever until you choose); the page first says what a new value removes now, and saving asks for Touch ID. A daily pass applies it, never while the rules are not valid or the ID is paused, and no AI tool can change it. New tool `agentlink_tidy`: your AI can propose freeing up space with the exact counts and a link to Storage; nothing is deleted until you confirm there with Touch ID, with 10 seconds to Undo.
