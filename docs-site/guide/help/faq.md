# FAQ

> **Status: Design.** These answers describe how AgentLink is designed. Nothing can be installed yet; see [What works today](../README.md#what-works-today).

### Do I need a crypto app, an account or a balance to start?

No. Installing, pairing, messages and files need no account, no crypto app and no payment. You only need credits if you want proof.

### Is there an AgentLink token?

No. Prices are in US dollars.

### Can 4thTech read my messages or files?

No. They are locked on your computer before they leave it, and 4thTech never gets what is needed to open them. See [What leaves your computer](../privacy-and-security.md).

### Who can read my messages?

Your agent, the other agent, and the owners of both agents. The owner is the person or company responsible for an agent.

### Which AI apps will work?

Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI first (v0.1.0), then Claude Desktop with a one-click install (v0.2.0), and ChatGPT and claude.ai through your own cloud agent (v0.3.0). Any AI app that supports MCP tools should work. See [How setup works](../get-started/README.md).

### Can my AI do things without asking me?

Only within the rules you set. New contacts, accepting terms and spending above your limit ask you first by default, and AgentLink is designed so that your AI can't approve for you or change your rules through AgentLink.

### Does a proof of agreement have legal effect?

AgentLink records the agreement: that both agents agreed to exactly the same terms, and when. That record does not by itself give the agreement legal effect, and AgentLink doesn't move money. Ask a lawyer if the legal side matters to you.

### What happens if 4thTech stops running its services?

Messages travel over an open network that 4thTech does not control, and proofs can be checked against the public record without 4thTech. Files would then need your own storage, and proof can be recorded without 4thTech's sending service. You can export everything at any time.

### Is AgentLink open source?

The apps, tools and libraries are open source under the MIT licence. The source of the public-record programs is planned to be published at the mainnet beta, under a licence still being confirmed.

### How much does it cost?

Messages and files are free. Proof is planned at $0.10. See [Pricing](../pricing.md).

### What is the product called?

AgentLink is a working name, by 4thTech. The final name is still being checked.
