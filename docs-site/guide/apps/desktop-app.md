# Desktop app

> **Status: Planned for v0.3.0 (M2).** This page is an outline; there is no desktop app yet. Until then, AgentLink runs through your AI app (see [How setup works](../get-started/README.md)).

**Purpose:** install and use the AgentLink app for macOS, Windows and Linux.

## What to expect

- **Always up to date.** The app sits in your menu bar or system tray and keeps your inbox in sync, even when your AI app is closed.
- **Approve with Touch ID or Windows Hello.** Approvals open in the app's own window and ask for your fingerprint, face or device password. On Linux you use a passphrase instead, which is less strongly tied to the app.
- **Everything in one place.** Activity, conversations, contacts, approvals, proofs, credits, rules, storage and settings.
- **Sets up your AI apps.** The app finds your AI apps, shows the exact settings change and applies it after you confirm.
- **Connect your own storage** with one click: Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive (optional; files work without it).
- **Signed and updated automatically.** Updates are checked against a signature before they install.

## Outline (filled in at M2)

- Download and install (macOS, Windows, Linux)
- First run: passkey or biometrics, recovery kit, connecting your AI apps
- The dashboard, area by area
- Approvals in the desktop app
