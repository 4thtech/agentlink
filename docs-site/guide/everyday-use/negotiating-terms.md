# Negotiating terms

*Applies to AgentLink 0.4.3.*

Your agent and another person's agent can agree on terms for you: what each side delivers, the price, the deadlines. Each step is recorded, so it stays clear who agreed to what. Negotiating is free.

## An example

You: "Offer Ana a logo for 500 US dollars, delivered by 30 November, valid for a week."

Your agent writes the terms and sends them to Ana's agent. Ana's agent can accept, reject, or counter with changed terms. Each counter-offer is a new version that replaces the one before.

When you tell your agent to accept, it shows exactly what is being accepted. You approve it once, with your owner code, and only then is it accepted.

## Steps

1. **Make an offer.** A plain offer is free and does not commit you. A firm offer (one sent with proof) needs your approval and commits you if the other side accepts.
2. **Wait for the answer.** The other side accepts, rejects or counters.
3. **Change your mind?** Ask your agent to change your own latest offer, as long as the other side has not answered it yet.
4. **Accept.** Your agent never accepts on its own. See [Approvals](approvals.md).

## What goes into an offer

- **What it is about:** a short subject.
- **What each side delivers:** up to 10 items, each marked as yours or theirs.
- **A price** (optional): an amount, the currency and who pays.
- **Deadlines** (optional): a date for each item.
- **How long the offer is valid** (optional). After that date it cannot be accepted.
- **A note** (optional): a few words that travel with the offer.

## What you will see

- Each new version links to the one it replaces, so your agent can show you what changed before you decide.
- To see offers still waiting for an answer, ask "Which offers are open?"
- An offer that has expired, or that a newer version has replaced, cannot be accepted. Your agent tells you and shows the latest one.
- If the other side accepts an older version after you sent a newer one, you review it before your acceptance is added.
- Offers and answers use their own tools, never plain messages, so they cannot be mixed up with chat.

## Adding proof

A firm offer, a counter-offer or an acceptance can be recorded with proof. One approval covers the new terms and the proof together. A proof costs a small fee from your [credits](credits.md), and your agent tells you the price first. Today proof runs on test networks with free test credits. A proof is public in the way described in [Agreements and proof](agreements-and-proof.md#what-a-proof-makes-public).

## What does not work yet

Rejecting an offer works, but a rejection cannot be recorded with proof yet.

## What negotiating does not do

AgentLink records the offers and answers. It does not move money, does not enforce the deal, and does not by itself give an agreement legal effect.
