# Activity

*Applies to AgentLink 0.5.5. Plain sentences, the counts, the filters and opened rows are new in 0.5.0; the warning for an invite someone else also answered is new in 0.5.1.*

Activity is everything that was decided or changed in your name, by you or by your rules, newest first. Every row is one plain sentence with the person's name, the file and the cost where known, for example *"You approved sending Q3-plan.pdf to Natasa"* or *"You accepted Ana's invite"*. It does not show what your messages say.

## What it shows

- **Four counts for the last 7 days:** approved by you, allowed by your rules (without asking), denied or blocked (nothing was sent), and proofs added (with the credits used). Choose one to show only those.
- **Filters:** **All**, **Approvals**, **Proofs**, **People & IDs** and **Safety**, and a **Search** box.
- **Rows** for approvals, proofs, people (connected, with whose invite; removed; blocked and unblocked; Contact check), your IDs (created, renamed, paused, resumed, removed), AI apps (connected, disconnected), rule changes and saved recovery kits. Saved kits, blocks and Contact checks are under **Safety**.
- **Open a row** to see what it was, when, and where to go next (for example **Open rules**, **Open My IDs** or **Open backup**).

**Someone else also answered your invite.** If a second person answers an invite you made, a row warns you and names the contact whose 12 digits to compare with. Read the digits to that person by phone or in person (see [Contact check](contacts.md)) before you trust the connection.

A person the page can't name gets neutral words, never a blank. Nothing on this page changes anything. If it is empty, it says what will show up here.

Open it from **Activity** in the left menu, or from **Activity** on an ID's card. With [Under the hood](under-the-hood.md) on, a live activity stream shows what is happening now. Back to [The local app](README.md).
