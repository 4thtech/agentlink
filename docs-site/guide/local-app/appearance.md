# Appearance and Under the hood

*Applies to AgentLink 0.4.6. It is not in 0.4.0.*

**Settings** is one list with a line under each setting. **Appearance** holds a few choices about how the app looks. They are kept in this browser only and are never sent anywhere.

The choices, in this order. Each group has its name written above it.

| Choice | Options |
|---|---|
| **Theme** | **System** (follows your computer), **Light** or **Dark** |
| **Reduce motion** | **System**, **On** or **Off**. **Off** still follows your computer if it asks for less motion |
| **Transparency** | **System** or **Reduce**, for solid panels instead of frosted ones |
| **Show Under the hood by default** | Off or on |

## Under the hood

Under the hood turns the same screens into tables with search, filters and sorting, and shows message IDs, exact times, sizes, receipts and proof. It is **off** by default and changes nothing about how the app works. Use the **Under the hood** switch at the top of any page (Cmd+Shift+U), or turn it on for every page in Appearance. The app remembers it for every window. Details: [Under the hood](under-the-hood.md).

It never shows a key, a code, a token or a private link.
