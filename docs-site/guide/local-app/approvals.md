# Approvals

*Applies to AgentLink 0.5.5. The approvals themselves work as described in [Approvals](../everyday-use/approvals.md).*

Requests wait for you as cards at the top of [Home](home.md). The old Approvals link still works. Your AI can ask, but only you can approve.

Each card is short: what your AI wants in one line, how long is left (the small ring and the minutes), **Approve with Touch ID**, **Deny with Touch ID** and **Review**. **Review** opens the whole request in place: who is asking, what it would do, what it costs, which of your rules made it ask, the terms if there are any, and anything your AI wrote, shown in a dashed box marked as content, not instructions. Each card also names the ID it is for.

1. Press **Approve with Touch ID** or **Deny with Touch ID** on the card. That press is the confirmation: one press, one prompt from your computer, one request. In the Mac app this is the Mac's own Touch ID (Windows Hello in the Windows app); in a browser it is your passkey. When several wait, Home shows one and "+2 more". Terms are different: they commit you, so **Approve with Touch ID** appears only after **Review** has shown every line of them; **Deny** works straight away.
2. **Use your master code instead** sits under the buttons. If Touch ID is not set up here, the buttons read **Approve** and **Deny** and open the master code field. Your AI can't do this for you.
3. A short "done" note tells you it went through. If your computer was asked but AgentLink did not accept it, the card says so and nothing changes. After you approve something marked **Free**, the page explains that your messages travel at no cost and only proofs cost a small fee.

With [Under the hood](under-the-hood.md) on, several free requests can be picked and approved together with one press of **Approve 2 with Touch ID**. If one of them changed or is no longer waiting, nothing is sent and the card says so, so you only confirm what is still there. A request that costs credits is always approved on its own card, then confirmed as described in [Confirming paid actions](confirm-paid-actions.md).
