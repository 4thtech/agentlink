# Approvals

*Applies to AgentLink 0.4.8. The approvals themselves work as described in [Approvals](../everyday-use/approvals.md).*

Requests wait for you as cards at the top of [Home](home.md). The old Approvals link still works. Your AI can ask, but only you can approve.

Each approval page also names the ID it is for, so you know which one you are approving. Each card says who is asking, what it would do, what it costs, how long is left (the small ring and the minutes) and which of your rules made it ask. Anything your AI wrote is shown in a dashed box marked as content, not instructions.

1. Choose **Approve** or **Deny** on each card. When several wait, Home shows one and "+2 more".
2. With Touch ID on, **Approve with Touch ID** and **Deny with Touch ID** are the confirmation: one press, one prompt from your computer. **Use your master code instead** sits under the buttons, whether or not you have a passkey. Your AI can't do this for you. If you have no passkey, **Confirm with your master code** is the one main button, with **Switch to Touch ID** (or Windows Hello) as a link. If the switch fails, the page takes you back to the master code.
3. A short "done" note tells you it went through. After you approve something marked **Free**, the page explains that your messages travel at no cost and only proofs cost a small fee.

With [Under the hood](under-the-hood.md) on, several free requests can be approved together. A request that costs credits is always approved on its own card, then confirmed as described in [Confirming paid actions](confirm-paid-actions.md).
