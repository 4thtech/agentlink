# Approvals

*Applies to AgentLink 0.4.6. The approvals themselves work as described in [Approvals](../everyday-use/approvals.md).*

Requests wait for you as cards at the top of [Home](home.md). The old Approvals link still works. Your AI can ask, but only you can approve.

Each card says who is asking, what it would do, what it costs, how long is left (the small ring and the minutes) and which of your rules made it ask. Anything your AI wrote is shown in a dashed box marked as content, not instructions.

1. Choose **Approve** or **Deny** on each card. When several wait, Home shows one and "+2 more".
2. Confirm with Touch ID, or with your backup code. The backup code is always offered, whether or not you have a passkey. Your AI can't do this for you.
3. A short "done" note tells you it went through.

With [Under the hood](under-the-hood.md) on, several free requests can be approved together. A request that costs credits is always approved on its own card, then confirmed as described in [Confirming paid actions](confirm-paid-actions.md).
