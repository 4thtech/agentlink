# AgentLink colours

Core brand colours. The full semantic palette for all four themes (dark, light and the two increased-contrast themes) is in `palette.json` under `themes`, and in the app design package as tokens.

| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Signal orange | `#FF6A1A` | 255, 106, 26 | 0/58/90/0 | Brand accent. The seal in the mark, primary actions, focus. |
| Graphite canvas | `#0B0C0E` | 11, 12, 14 | 21/14/0/95 | Dark background. |
| Graphite surface | `#111316` | 17, 19, 22 | 23/14/0/91 | Panels; app icon background. |
| Graphite raised | `#171A1E` | 23, 26, 30 | 23/13/0/88 | Cards, menus. |
| Ink light | `#E7E9EC` | 231, 233, 236 | 2/1/0/7 | Mark brackets and text on dark. |
| Ink dark | `#0E1013` | 14, 16, 19 | 26/16/0/93 | Mark brackets and text on light. |
| Paper | `#F5F6F7` | 245, 246, 247 | 1/0/0/3 | Light background. |
| Proof violet | `#A495FF` | 164, 149, 255 | 36/42/0/0 | Proof seal on dark (#5B4BD6 on light). |
| Success teal | `#4FD1A5` | 79, 209, 165 | 62/0/21/18 | Success on dark (#0A6E51 text on light). |
| Info blue | `#6CB6FF` | 108, 182, 255 | 58/29/0/0 | Info and verified domain on dark. |
| Warning amber | `#F2C94C` | 242, 201, 76 | 0/17/69/5 | Warnings on dark. |
| Danger red | `#FF6B6B` | 255, 107, 107 | 0/58/58/0 | Destructive and integrity failures only. |

CMYK values are straight conversions for reference only; proof print colour against a physical swatch. Signal orange is a screen colour; in one-colour print use the black or white logo.

The 4thTech green `#2CD8A7` belongs to the endorsing brand and is not part of the AgentLink palette.
