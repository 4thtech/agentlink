## Decision

**Owner-operation bodies (G-29).** Owner operations use one owner-signed object, `SignedOwnerAction` (04 §2.5.1), produced by the local UI, the CLI and the console; the `approval`, `policy` and `revoke` envelopes only carry it, and 04 §3.4.1 fixes the bodies and receiver rules. The object binds to one agent by its canonical EVM address. **Revocation is permanent per canonical agent EVM address** (every epoch, no un-revoke), and a contact accepts a `revoke` directly from the owner key after verifying the signature against the owner key bound in its stored card of that agent, whatever the envelope's `from`. Confirmed by Tali (owner's merge of #10, 2026-10-14; stated in chat)
