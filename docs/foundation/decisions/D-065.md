# D-65: Two amendments of D-58 for owner-copy relays (G-24)

- **Status:** Accepted (Tali, 2026-10-14, at merge of 4thtech/agentlink#51), with every G-24 value in 07 §4.3 and the pointers in 04 and 08
- **Date:** 2026-10-14
- **Specs:** 04, 07
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**Two amendments of D-58 for owner-copy relays (G-24).** (1) **Owner-channel `card-update`:** besides the `card-update` to each contact, the core sends one to its own owner (recipients exactly the agent and its owner) for its first card at `init` and on every new card, even with no contacts; it is re-published every 24 h during a relay-change overlap and every 7 days otherwise, to the old list, the new list and the release default relay set, unless the owner turns that off for the agent under Advanced. The card inside is owner-signed with presence; only core flows create it, never an MCP tool (I-4). `card-update` therefore has two valid recipient sets (04 §3.4.1, 07 §4.3). (2) **`relays` in `handshake-ack`:** the inviter's list exactly as in the invite, checked against the invite and `card.relaysHash`, so the invitee's owner can reach the inviter, for example with a revoke (04 §3.4.1; types and vectors in M1-2d)
