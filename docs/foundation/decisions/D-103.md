# D-103: Amends D-100: `agentlink policy recover` signs above both the mark and the stored version

- **Status:** Decided (owner, 19 Oct 2026)
- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Specs:** 06
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**Amends D-100: `agentlink policy recover` signs above both the mark and the stored version.** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-19:** "amend D-100 to sign above both mark and stored version". The recovered policy is a complete `policy.set` at version max(mark, stored) + 1, where `stored` is the version of the stored policy when it parses (otherwise only the mark counts). A policy signed at mark + 1 alone could sit at or below a stored version that is ahead of the mark (`mark_mismatch`) and fail closed again. Everything else in D-100 stands. Spec: 06 §3, §8.
