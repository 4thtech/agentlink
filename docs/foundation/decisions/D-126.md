## Decision

**Mechanical merge of `origin/main` into an approved PR head: lightweight re-verification.** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-23** (adopting the orchestrator's recommendation, to speed up merging; owner-confirmed 2026-10-23). *Orchestrator wording, not the owner's words:* when an approved PR's head moves only by a mechanical merge of `origin/main`, the fresh independent review may be a lightweight automated verification by a fresh-context worker (any model, for example Haiku). It checks exactly three facts: `git show --remerge-diff <merge>` is empty (no hand conflict resolution), the PR's own diff against `main` is byte-identical to the previously approved head's diff, and required CI is green. It records `IR-<ID>-<n>: PASS (mechanical) at <sha>`. Any non-empty remerge-diff, any change to the PR's own diff, or any S2 conflict resolution still needs a full independent review (Opus; Fable for keys, crypto, money or contracts). `review-gate` accepts the `(mechanical)` marker unchanged. Process change: `audit-rules.md` §4.6, `delivery-process.md` step 5.
