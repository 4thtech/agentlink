## Decision

**G-21: the M2 gate's onboarding timing rule (median, 80% share, 15-minute ceiling).** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-23:** the M2 gate rule is: median install → first message under 5 minutes AND at least 80% of testers under 5 minutes AND no tester over 15 minutes for a cause on our side. (owner-confirmed 2026-10-23.) *Orchestrator wording, not the owner's words:* an over-15 caused by us is a bug that must be fixed before the gate passes; time spent waiting for the counterpart is excluded; timing is self-reported and opt-in with no analytics, by the same method as M1 (D-124, 08 §2.3). The M1 gate stays strict per D-124; this rule replaces the strict reading for M2-GATE only. Cards: M2-GATE.
