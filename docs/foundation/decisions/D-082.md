## Decision

**Owner seed blobs `ALKP` (passkey PRF) and `ALKR` (recovery code) (M1-1k).** Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-17 ("A B"). 04 §2.3 named the passkey PRF wrap key and the recovery-code KDF but no blob layout, nonce or AAD. (A) **`ALKP` v1** (04 §2.3.1): magic, version, `aead`, the owner's x-only Nostr key, the WebAuthn credential ID (u16be length, 1..1023), a random 12-byte nonce and AES-256-GCM over the 32-byte seed; AAD = every header byte; size checked before decryption; the seed must derive the header owner key; one blob per passkey, used in IndexedDB, the relay backup and the localhost passkey (M1-8e). The `wrapKey` derivation is unchanged. (B) **`ALKR` v1** (04 §2.3.2): the `ALKO` layout with its own magic and HKDF label `agentlink/v1/owner-recovery-wrap`, generated 120-bit codes only (`secretKind` = 1), Argon2id m = 64 MiB, t = 3, p = 1 with the `ALKS` floor and cap. `ALKP`, `ALKR`, `ALKO` and `ALKS` never open as one another. Order: spec, then `prf-wrap` vectors (sealed blobs, tampering, cross-type), then the M1-1k code.
