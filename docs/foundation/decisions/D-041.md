- **Specs:** none

## Decision

**The desktop apps (macOS, Windows, Linux) are full local dashboards:** activity, threads, contacts, approvals, proofs, credits, policies, storage and settings. They share `packages/ui` with the web console, plus the tray and native approval window from D-36
