# D-62: Envelope signatures, keys and limits (G-2), with the contract rules stated alongside D-61

- **Status:** Accepted (Tali, 2026-10-14, at merge of 4thtech/agentlink#34), except the held-envelope cap of at most 100 per contact owner (04 §3.1.1 step 7), which stays proposed (owner confirms). Proposed amendment (G-9; owner confirms at merge): held envelopes have their own pool, outside the requests-queue limits (07 §3.2)
- **Date:** 2026-10-14
- **Specs:** 04, 05
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**Envelope signatures, keys and limits (G-2), with the contract rules stated alongside D-61.** BIP-340 envelope signatures over a tagged `envelopeHash`, one tag per object a Nostr key signs; the agent key signs agent envelopes and the owner key only `approval`, `policy` and `revoke` in a presence session; key interfaces in `@agentlink/crypto` with a shared `EnvelopeKeys`; the owner read path and where an owner copy is shown; nine fail-closed verification steps; size limits and where each is enforced (04 §3.1.1). Also: a signed Record or Receipt is replaced only when the chain shows it can never land (04 §5); receipt part states (04 §3.4.2); the receipt reading of "identical" (05 §3.3); pause and unpause still work after freeze (05 §5.1); replacement reads at the `finalized` block; at most 100 held envelopes per contact owner (proposed; not covered by the merge confirmation); G-46 timing: the construction is fixed before M1-7b becomes `ready` and before the `aead-envelope` and `envelope-sig` vectors freeze
