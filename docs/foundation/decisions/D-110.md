# D-110: Solana support after EVM coverage (direction only)

- **Status:** Decided (owner, 20 Oct 2026; direction only)
- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Specs:** 07
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**Solana support after EVM coverage (direction only).** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-20:** "Also, another issue that I thought of after we will deploy and cover all the EVM chains, we must also develop smart contracts for Solana and enable agent link on Solana." After the EVM chains on the roadmap are deployed (Base and Gnosis in M3, then the M4 EVM chains), AgentLink adds Solana: on-chain programs equivalent to the Notary and fee vault, and client support. Planned as M6, after M4; no dates are committed. Nothing is decided beyond the direction: a design spike (card M6-1) answers the open questions first, among them the program language and framework and the audit path (D-11 licence, an independent contract audit), ed25519 versus secp256k1 agent keys and how agent cards and proof of possession (D-102) bind a Solana key, the account and rent model against fees and credits (12), USDC on Solana and the USD price list (D-45, D-46), the loss of the "same addresses on every chain" property, automatic chain selection and proof verification for a non-EVM chain (07 §6.3, §8), the D-109 privacy properties on Solana, and the rule that users never pick a chain (D-39, D-40). Spec: 10 M6.
