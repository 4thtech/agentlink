# D-47: Crypto-only charging during beta

- **Status:** Decided (12 Oct 2026)
- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Specs:** none
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**Crypto-only charging during beta**, no fiat. The legal entity is to be non-EU (D-15). Counsel must assess EU VAT exposure before paid mainnet launch (M3-7): B2C digital services to EU consumers may fall under non-Union OSS even for non-EU sellers, and crypto payment does not remove VAT
