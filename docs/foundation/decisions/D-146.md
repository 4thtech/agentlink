## Decision

**Names for the three codes and tags (option A): "master code", "Contact check", "ID tag".** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-11-12** (UX audit of 12 Nov, naming recommendation option A, confirmed explicitly). (1) **Master code** replaces "backup code" (local app) and "owner code" (terminal) in every user-facing text. It is the same generated 24-character secret as today: it unlocks the owner key and approves owner actions when Touch ID or Windows Hello can't be used (restoring the owner key stays the recovery code's job). Proposed copy (WP-9): "Use your master code instead" on every confirm screen, and a purpose line. (2) **Contact check** replaces "safety code" as the name of the step: the 12 digits both sides see at pairing (04 §3.6.1), optional, compared once by phone; the digits are "the 12 digits", never a "code". Proposed line (WP-9): "Optional. Read these 12 digits to {name} by phone. If they match, nobody tampered with your invite. Nothing to save." The identity label "Safety code checked" (11 §5.2) follows in the sweep. (3) **Owner fingerprint** is hidden in the Simple view and called **ID tag** under the hood. Proposed line (WP-9): "Tells apart two contacts with the same name. Nothing to save." Where a name must fall back (11 §5.2 rule 3) the hood form stays `Contact 7F3A·19C2`. Code identifiers (`ownerCode`, `backupCode`, `safetyCode`), data, protocol and the spec's technical sections keep their names; formats, secrecy and behaviour are unchanged. One rename sweep (UX1112 wave 2, WP-9) changes user-facing strings, docs-site, knowledge, skills and the tests that pin them, with a vocabulary test banning the old words in the default UI. Amends the user-facing words of D-67, D-130 (1) and G-28. Spec: 11 §5.5.
