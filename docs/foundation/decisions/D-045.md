## Decision

**Economic model (public, `12-economics-and-fees.md`).** One USD price list, the same on every chain. Beta prices: proof (record incl. up to 3 receipts) **$0.10**, on-chain cap $0.50. Verified registration **$5 one-time**, cap $25. Anchor, deal and disclose are priced later. Talk, files, pairing and verification are free; testnet is free via faucet. Price changes inside the cap need a Safe signature and are **announced ≥ 7 days ahead** (a policy commitment). Cap raises need a 7-day on-chain delay. Quotes show USD with the protocol and integrator split
