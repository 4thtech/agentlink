# Support

AgentLink is **pre-alpha**. Help is best-effort and there is no service level.

- **Setup problem?** Run `npx -y @4thtech-io/agentlink doctor`. It checks your setup and prints the fix for anything that is wrong. Then see the [user guide](docs-site/guide/README.md) and its FAQ.
- **Found a bug, or have a question or idea?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/4thtech/agentlink/issues). Use test data only, and never paste keys, owner codes or private messages.
- **Understand how it works?** [Technical docs](docs-site/technical/README.md).
- **Want to help build?** See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Security problem?** Do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
