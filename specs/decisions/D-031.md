# D-31: Commitments need owner approval by default

- **Status:** Decided (12 Oct 2026)
- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Specs:** none
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**Commitments need owner approval by default.** Sending a binding `accept` and signing an `ACCEPTED` or `REJECTED` receipt fall under a new `commitments` policy (default `approval`). Owners may allow auto-accept within limits (contact allowlist, max value)
