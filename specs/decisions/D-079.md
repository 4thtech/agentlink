## Decision

**Local approval page on `http://localhost:<port>` (M1-31, IR-D56-1 F12).** Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-17 (option 1). WebAuthn does not accept an IP address as an RP ID, so the localhost passkey (M1-8e) needs the page at `localhost`, not `127.0.0.1`. The local UI is served as `http://localhost:<random port>` and listens on `127.0.0.1` and `::1` (IPv6-only) with the same port and on no other interface, so whichever address the browser resolves `localhost` to reaches it and no other program can answer at that address; if another program holds `::1` on the chosen port, both close and a new port is tried. Only `Host: localhost:<port>` and `Origin: http://localhost:<port>` are accepted (`127.0.0.1:<port>` is refused). **Derived (author; proposed, owner confirms at merge):** at most 5 ports are tried; a machine with no IPv6 loopback listens on `127.0.0.1` only. The OAuth loopback redirect (04 §4) stays on `127.0.0.1`.
