## Decision

**Privacy of on-chain proofs.** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-20:** "Yes, let's write all the recommendations in the specifications, and also let's build this into the roadmap so we know what we need to add, because I think privacy is extremely important and we need to think about how we can protect our users." Taken from the 2026-10-20 privacy audit of the public record. Content stays private today; metadata does not. (1) **Disclosure.** Every proof surface says in plain words what a proof makes public: that the agent recorded something, when, and its kind; once a receipt is posted, who it went to; never the content (11 §5.6; 06 §2.4; docs site). (2) **Delivered receipts off by default.** `receipts.autoDelivered` defaults to `false` (was `true`), which strengthens privacy; a contact rule can turn it on for one contact, a sender can ask for a receipt in the `proof` body, and the recipient's agent signs one only under policy or when its user asks; OPENED stays explicit (06 §2.4, §3; 04 §3.4, §5; 07 §8). (3) **Delayed or batched receipts** (an optional random delay window, batched with other receipts at the submitter): specified as optional, M2 (04 §5.1). (4) **`parentRecordId` rule.** Zero by default; set only to the agent's own earlier record, never to another signer's record unless both sides agreed it in terms (05 §3.1; 04 §5.1). (5) **Per-relationship signing addresses**: one derived signing address per contact relationship (or per proof), bound to the agent card by proof of possession (D-102); design goal and constraints in 04 §5.1, scheduled for M2, before the contracts are audited. (6) **Funding linkage**: user warning and guidance (free test credits, a separate top-up source, gasless top-ups), 12 §4.4 and 11 §5.6. (7) **Registry (M2)**: no `nameHash` or any other guessable identifier in public events or calldata; the agent-to-owner link is public only if the owner registers (05 §4). (8) **Submitter and relays**: no IP or address logging, Cloudflare-side logging off for these Workers, and the submitter never sees salts before a receipt, keys or plaintext, stated as an invariant (07 §7). (9) **Proof pack disclosure**: the user guide lists what sharing a pack reveals (07 §8). Roadmap: M1 disclosure copy, receipts off and the `parentRecordId` rule (M1-33a–c); M2 per-relationship addresses, registry privacy, delayed receipts (M2-17a–c); ongoing: the service logging policy (OPS-PRIV-1). **Older policies (decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-20: "Let's go with your recommendation"):** the old `true` default is treated as not chosen; the next owner-signed `policy.set` for any reason carries `false` unless the owner explicitly keeps `true`, and until then `agentlink doctor` shows: "Delivery receipts are on from an older default; they will turn off unless you keep them." (06 §3). Derived details are proposed; the owner confirms at merge.
