# D-112: "Users pay all costs" covers proofs, receipts and top-ups only; messages and files stay free

- **Status:** Decided (owner, 21 Oct 2026)
- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Specs:** 12
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**"Users pay all costs" covers proofs, receipts and top-ups only; messages and files stay free.** **Decided by the owner, 2026-10-21**, answering open question (b) of D-111: "keep messages and files free; 'users pay all costs' covers proofs, receipts and top-ups only." Consistent with D-45. D-45 to D-50 stand: messages and files, including the encrypted drop, stay free to the user, and 4thTech pays drop storage (D-48, 12 §2). D-111 point 7 applies to proofs, receipts and top-ups: fees, network costs and receipts are user-paid and 4thTech subsidises none (the testnet faucet excepted). The network cost of gasless top-ups (D-111 (a)) stays open. Spec: 12 §1, §2; `docs/design/topup-ux.md`.
