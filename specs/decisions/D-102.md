# D-102: M1-4d-d: agent-card proof of possession in proof packs

- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**M1-4d-d: agent-card proof of possession in proof packs.** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-19:** "A A A: per-card signatures, carry them in handshake and card updates, contract accounts later on a card". (1) The agent's EVM key (`cardAgentSig`) and its message key (`cardPop`, BIP-340 over `taggedHash("agentlink/card-pop/v1", sha256(JCS(card)))`) each sign the card; both are fixed per card, never per pack. (2) A contact's `cardAgentSig` and `cardPop` travel in `handshake`, `handshake-ack` and `card-update`, so a pack the recipient exports can verify. (3) Contract-account (ERC-1271) signers are not confirmed through a card in this version; a later card (M1-4d-e) covers them. Without the signatures a pack stays `record_only` (`card_pop_missing`); a forged or moved signature fails it (`card_pop_invalid`); both valid → `verified`. Spec: 04 §2.9, §3.4.1, §3.6; 07 §3, §8.
