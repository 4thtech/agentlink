# D-96: M1-4d-c: a contract-account signer that no longer confirms its signature is a non-fatal finding

- **Status:** Decided (owner, 19 Oct 2026)
- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Specs:** 05, 07, 11
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**M1-4d-c: a contract-account signer that no longer confirms its signature is a non-fatal finding.** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-19.** When the record signer is a contract account (ERC-1271) and its `isValidSignature` does not accept the pack's signature at verification time, the verifier reports its own non-fatal code, `signer_no_longer_confirms`, and leaves the verdict unchanged (`verified` or `record_only`): the proof network checked the signature when it stored the record (05 §3.2), and the account's rules may have changed since. User wording (11 §5): "The signer's account no longer confirms this record. The proof network checked it when the record was made." Spec: 07 §8.
