- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**Recipient-side proof-pack export (J8 "both sides export").** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-19:** "Yes, definitely the recipient should be able to export proof pack go as recommended." (1) The `proof` body (04 §3.4) also carries the signed `record` (the Record struct in its JSON form, 05 §3.1) and its `signature`, present together or not at all. The body travels end to end encrypted to the recipient only; nothing new goes to any 4thTech service. (2) Before storing them, the recipient checks them against the on-chain record and the envelope it received (struct hash, `recordId`, signer, kind, its own commitment and the signature); any mismatch refuses the whole `proof` body, fail closed, like the existing proof-body checks. (3) `exportProofPack` builds a pack on the recipient side from the stored Record, with `recipient` left out unless `includeRecipient`, as on the sender side (M1-7g). (4) A `proof` body from an older sender without the two fields is still accepted, and recipient export returns `RECORD_UNAVAILABLE`. Spec: 04 §3.4; 07 §8. Card M1-7g-b (S2).
