## Decision

**Default relay set (owner decision of Tali, 15 Oct 2026; M1-18).** The release's default relays are the public Nostr relays `wss://nos.lol`, `wss://relay.primal.net`, `wss://relay.snort.social` and `wss://nostr.mom`, in that order, for the J9 dogfood and early production. `relay.damus.io` was first named and is dropped: its vetting on 15 Oct 2026 showed that a `#p` read, the shape our sync uses, needs NIP-42 AUTH and that its AUTH login is refused ("relay needs serviceUrl to be configured"), so a sync against it returns nothing; the four chosen relays accept kind 1059 and serve the `#p` read without AUTH. 4thTech runs its own relay (strfry, 30-day kind-1059 retention, AUTH-gated reads, no content logging) once there are first concrete users, and adds it to the list then by a new decision. The list lives in one place, `DEFAULT_RELAYS` in `@agentlink/transport-nostr` (`src/default-relays.ts`); every surface (owner init, the bootstrap set of 07 §4.3, the card) imports it and none keeps a copy. It holds 4 entries, within the 2 to 16 of 04 §3.4; each is wss, passes the relay URL rule of 04 §4, and a test guards the single source. Relays are public and untrusted for content: they see only ciphertext gift wraps (I-3). They are vetted with the opt-in `tools/relays/vet.mjs` and the checklist in `project/runbooks/relay-vetting.md` (kind 1059 accepted and read back, NIP-11 `max_message_length` of at least 90 112 against the 88 KB largest wrap frame of 04 §3.1.1, retention, NIP-42); M1-18 is signed off only when the checklist is green.

## Amendment (0.5.13, RELAY-DEFAULTS-SWAP and RELAY-CARD-MIGRATION; owner decision of Tali, 18 Nov 2026)

**The default set is now `wss://offchain.pub`, `wss://relay.primal.net`, `wss://relay.snort.social` and `wss://nostr.bitcoiner.social`, in that order.** `wss://nos.lol` and `wss://nostr.mom` leave it because some networks cannot reach them. Everything else above stands: one source (`DEFAULT_RELAYS`), 4 entries, the relay URL rule of 04 §4, the vetting runbook. The daily `relay-health` workflow, and its run on a pull request that changes the list, vet the set read-only from CI (`project/runbooks/relay-vetting.md`).

- **Legacy reads.** The two dropped relays stay in `LEGACY_READ_RELAYS`: read-only, never published to on their own, never logged in to. They are kept at least until 2027-03-02 and removed by a later release (card LEGACY-RELAYS-REMOVAL).
- **Card-update fan-out.** Until 2027-01-31 a card-update to a contact is also published to the legacy relays, and it counts as sent only on an acknowledgement from a relay that is not a legacy relay.
- **Moving an existing ID.** An ID whose card lists a dropped relay moves to the current set with one owner approval ("Update your contact details" in the app, `agentlink owner update-contact-details` in the terminal): a rotation to the next key epoch signed with owner presence. No AI tool can start or approve it. Spec: 04 §2.6; 07 §4.3.

## Amendment (0.5.17, RELAY-DEFAULTS-SWAP-2; owner decision of Tali, 21 Nov 2026)

**The default set is now `wss://nostr.data.haus`, `wss://relay.primal.net`, `wss://relay.snort.social` and `wss://nostr.bitcoiner.social`, in that order.** `wss://offchain.pub` leaves it ("Swap now") and joins `LEGACY_READ_RELAYS` (read only, never published to). Legacy dates are unchanged: card-update fan-out until 2027-01-31, removal not before 2027-03-02.

- **Reason.** offchain.pub allows about 2 connections per computer: with 2 held, 11 of 12 new connects were refused (port 80 still accepted, so it is a firewall limit). AgentLink opens a pool per process (one per AI app plus the app), so users could be refused there.
- **Vetting (21 Nov 2026, `tools/relays/vet.mjs`, 10 candidates).** Only `wss://nostr.data.haus` passed: 5 of 5 concurrent connections, connect 630 to 700 ms, publish accepted, read by `#p` without AUTH, read by id (read-back served), NIP-11 shows no auth and no NIP-42, max message 131 072 (90 112 needed). Rejected: `relay.nostr.net` (the first candidate: `#p` reads need NIP-42 AUTH that it cannot complete), `relay.damus.io` (3 of 5 connections, `#p` read failed), `nostr.einundzwanzig.space` (publish rejected), `nostr-01.yakihonne.com` (`#p` read failed), `nostr.oxtr.dev` (0 of 5), `relay.nostr.bg` and `relay.0xchat.com` (no DNS), `nostr.fmt.wiz.biz` (NIP-11 error), `relay.mostr.pub` (301), `relay.nostr.band` (down), `nostr-pub.wellorder.net` (503).
- **Moving an existing ID.** Same flow as 0.5.13: "Update your contact details", once, one owner approval. Spec: 07 §4.3; runbook `project/runbooks/relay-vetting.md`.
- **Re-checks.** 24 h re-check 22 Nov 2026: PASS (still there at 1449 min); 7-day re-check due 28 Nov 2026.
