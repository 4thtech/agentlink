## Decision

**Default relay set (owner decision of Tali, 15 Oct 2026; M1-18).** The release's default relays are the public Nostr relays `wss://nos.lol`, `wss://relay.primal.net`, `wss://relay.snort.social` and `wss://nostr.mom`, in that order, for the J9 dogfood and early production. `relay.damus.io` was first named and is dropped: its vetting on 15 Oct 2026 showed that a `#p` read, the shape our sync uses, needs NIP-42 AUTH and that its AUTH login is refused ("relay needs serviceUrl to be configured"), so a sync against it returns nothing; the four chosen relays accept kind 1059 and serve the `#p` read without AUTH. 4thTech runs its own relay (strfry, 30-day kind-1059 retention, AUTH-gated reads, no content logging) once there are first concrete users, and adds it to the list then by a new decision. The list lives in one place, `DEFAULT_RELAYS` in `@agentlink/transport-nostr` (`src/default-relays.ts`); every surface (owner init, the bootstrap set of 07 §4.3, the card) imports it and none keeps a copy. It holds 4 entries, within the 2 to 16 of 04 §3.4; each is wss, passes the relay URL rule of 04 §4, and a test guards the single source. Relays are public and untrusted for content: they see only ciphertext gift wraps (I-3). They are vetted with the opt-in `tools/relays/vet.mjs` and the checklist in `docs/runbooks/relay-vetting.md` (kind 1059 accepted and read back, NIP-11 `max_message_length` of at least 90 112 against the 88 KB largest wrap frame of 04 §3.1.1, retention, NIP-42); M1-18 is signed off only when the checklist is green.
