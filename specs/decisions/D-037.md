# D-37: Two directions

- **Status:** Decided (12 Oct 2026)
- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Specs:** none
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**Two directions:** local (npx in M1, then desktop apps) and cloud. In the cloud, users self-host their always-on agent in their **own** Cloudflare account, and 4thTech hosts only zero-knowledge parts (console, verify, invite, pay, submitter). A 4thTech-hosted remote MCP stays approval-mode only, P2/M4, after design review
