## Decision

**Payment model: prepaid credits, QR top-up now, a Withdraw credits button, public trust documentation.** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-22:** "Okay, I agree with all recommendations … let's give user withdrawal credit button". *Orchestrator wording, not the owner's words:* the payment model stays as designed (self-custodial agent account, prepaid withdrawable credits, the submitter pays network fees, gasless USDC top-up); pay-by-card through a provider comes later when the legal entity exists; until then a simple QR top-up (scan to send funds to the agent's address on the named network, then top up); a Withdraw credits button under Advanced on the Proofs page (owner presence); privacy guidance as in 12 §4 and 11 §5.6; the public docs (README, docs guide and technical pages, GitBook) explain all of this with diagrams, including a trust bridge for the private contracts (card DOCS-PAY-TRUST).
