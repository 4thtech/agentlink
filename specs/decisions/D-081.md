## Decision

**G-20: Revocation typed data and owner nonces.** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-17 (option A).** 05 §4 adds `struct Revocation { address agentEvm; uint32 keyEpoch; uint256 nonce; uint64 deadline; }`, signed by `ownerEvm` (EIP-712, OCC v2 chain domain; ERC-1271 accepted); `revoke` takes `(Revocation, ownerSig)`. `Registration` and `Revocation` nonces are random, single-use per owner, tracked in `usedNonces[ownerEvm][nonce]` and shared by both types. Revoke reverts if expired, used, or `keyEpoch` ≠ stored epoch, and works while paused (as D-59). **Derived (review IR-215-1; owner confirms at merge):** the owner decision named `uint64 keyEpoch`; the type is aligned to the stored `uint32` (04 §2.4 card, 05 §6 `AgentRegistered`); the signer must equal the stored `ownerEvm`; revoking an unknown or already-revoked agent reverts; new errors `NonceUsed()`, `EpochMismatch()`, `AgentNotRegistered()`, `AlreadyRevoked()`, with `Expired()` and `InvalidSignature()` reused (05 §7). Unblocks the M1-1i/M1-1j revocation vectors and M2-4a (implemented in the private contracts repo). **Decided by Tali, 2026-10-18 (previously proposed):** the `Revocation` JSON form as proposed (05 §3.1 style: lowercase `agentEvm`, decimal-string `nonce`, JSON-integer `keyEpoch` and `deadline`, exactly four keys); `keyEpoch` is `uint32`.
