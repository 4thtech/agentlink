- **Specs:** 07, 12
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**Who pays for receipts: the sender's proof fee covers up to 3; deferred posting as the fallback.** **Decided by the owner, 2026-10-21** (M1-34): "B: the sender's proof fee covers up to 3 receipts; D as fallback when the submitter is unreachable; a recipient is never charged without asking." Option B (card M1-34): the sender's proof fee already covers up to 3 receipts, and the submitter accepts up to 3 receipts for a record whose fee was charged, whatever the recipient holds, capped per record. Option D (card M1-34): the receipt is queued locally and posted when possible; the proof stays valid without it, with fewer receipts. Options A (recipient pays) and C (4thTech-paid) are not adopted; C was already excluded by D-111. Consistent with D-111 point 7 (users pay every cost) and D-112. Not yet built: the submitter side is card M1-34-b. Spec: 12 §2, §11; 07 §7; `docs/design/topup-ux.md` §8.
