# D-50: Revenue operations, phased

- **Status:** Decided (13 Oct 2026): phased plan; details at M3/M4
- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Specs:** 12
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**Revenue operations, phased.** (1) Beta: fees accrue in `OCCFeeVault` and are swept (permissionlessly) to the treasury Safe per chain; visibility from explorers, the Safe UI and a **monthly revenue report** (CSV from `Charged`, `Deposited`, `Withdrawn` and `Swept` events per chain, M3); bookkeeping stays simple because fees are stablecoin-only (D-46). (2) Post-beta (M4): an **internal finance dashboard** in the private `agentlink-ops` repo, read-only over the on-chain events (revenue by day, chain, fee type and `appId`; payers; credit flows; treasury balances; sweep status; current fees and caps; alerts). It holds no keys: fee and treasury changes are only drafted as Safe transaction proposals and signed in the Safe. `DECISION NEEDED (owner)`: the indexer. (3) **Invoicing via opt-in billing profiles:** a business signs once with its owner key to link its agent addresses to billing details, after which its on-chain charges can be invoiced; everyone else stays pseudonymous. Identity hooks: OCCRegistry agent → owner (M2) and domain verification (D-34). Billing data is held only by 4thTech ops and never includes content (zero access holds); data protection and VAT reviewed by counsel (D-47). Fiat payments stay out of scope until legal is settled (12 §7.1)
