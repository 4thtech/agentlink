## Decision

**Local app v2 design decisions, and confirming paid actions with the computer's own verification.** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-27:** (1) every decision of design brief B-11 v2 §18 as recommended (option (a) of decisions 1 to 6), including Messages and Files In/Out as sketched in §17 (read-only); (2) AgentLink is not a financial app, wallet or bank: the only paid actions are a proof ($0.10) and verified registration ($5.00), there are no payments between agents, and 4thTech never holds credits; (3) the owner confirms paid actions with whatever the computer offers (Touch ID or another fingerprint reader, Windows Hello, a passkey or a PIN) through platform user verification, in one polished confirmation sheet (amount, what it is for, the system prompt, a progress-to-check moment, a reduced-motion version), with no charts, balance hero or other finance-app features. (4) on 2026-10-27, brief §20 as recommended: the sheet is used for verified registration, a proof above the signed limits (or set to "Ask me") and any change to the limits, while proofs inside the limits stay automatic; the backup code stays a small secondary link on the sheet; the sheet closes itself about 1.2 s after Done. *Orchestrator wording, proposed, owner confirms at merge:* the ceremony rules (04 §2.5.3 rules 1 to 5 and 7; 12 §4.6, 06 §3, 08 §1; brief §19). 11 §11 item 6 now states the motion range of decision 2.
