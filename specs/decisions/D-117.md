## Decision

**Design decisions: backup, exchange network, "wallet", minimum; and the UX north star.** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-21:** "In regards to design decisions, let's go with your recommendations. Please remember that we are aiming for best possible UI and UX, We are also aiming to abstract as many complexity as possible". Answers the open questions of `docs/design/adjustments.md` (D-116): (A) *accepted recommendation:* backup in the local UI: export on the local page behind the owner's passkey; restore stays in the terminal only (06 §8, 04 §2.3); *amended by D-147 (12 Nov 2026): restore also in the app.* (B) *accepted recommendation:* the pay page shows the exchange's network only inside the exchange steps, as information, never as a choice (11 §14 Q5, option A). (C) *accepted recommendation:* "wallet" never appears in our labels (11 §5.1; `topup-ux.md` §7 #3). (D) *accepted recommendation:* crypto top-ups (exchange and connect) have a $5 minimum, and the card minimum stays open (the provider's, M2-18a); the $0.10 in proof copy is the price of a proof, not a top-up minimum; below it the page says "send at least $X more and your agent adds it all" (`topup-ux.md` §6, §7 #6). *Already settled by the spec, restated and not decided here:* the update banner keeps the 11 §6 Updates string; the console never restores a computer's recovery kit (04 §2.3, 06 §8). **UX north star (owner's words):** "best possible UI and UX … abstract as many complexity as possible". Where a design choice has a simpler path for the user and a more complex one, take the simpler one and let the agent carry the complexity; this guides every UI card and every Claude Design brief (D-39, D-40). One consequence recorded in `adjustments.md` B-4: credential entry for own storage stays in the terminal (06 §8 `storage connect`); the local page shows status and a "connect in terminal" step. Spec: `docs/design/adjustments.md`, `docs/design/topup-ux.md`, 08 §1, 11 §5.1.
