# D-150: The owner attaches light text files in the app's conversation and sends them himself

- **Status:** Proposed, owner confirms at merge (the owner's wish, 13 Nov 2026: attach light files, Markdown first, in the conversation)
- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Specs:** 04, 08
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**The owner attaches light text files in the app's conversation and sends them himself (ATT-APP-ATTACH; amends "sending from the app stays later work" of D-149 and "To reply, ask your AI." of 08 §1).** (1) In a conversation with a connected, active contact: **Attach** (file picker) and drag-and-drop; files wait as chips with Remove and an optional note; **Send** sends them in one message. Types `.md`, `.markdown`, `.txt`, `.json` only (Markdown first), at most 8 MiB each and per message, at most 5 files, valid UTF-8 without NUL; the name through core's `sanitizeFileName`, then the allowlist. (2) A new local route kind, `POST /api/ui/attach` (`presence: 'attach'`): the session, CSRF, Origin, fetch-metadata and octet-stream gate of every write, no operation input or presence header; a declared length, and the rate and parallel limits taken before the body is read; a page request ID as core's `clientRequestId`, so a retry does not send twice; not an MCP tool and not exported from the package entry, so no AI can call it (I-4). (3) The files are written into the ID's own Send folder only (D-121 D6; never another allowed folder; pinned realpath, exclusive create, no link followed, no overwrite: `name (2).md`), then sent through core's ordinary send under the policy in force (inline or the drop; type from the extension). A message not sent removes what it wrote. (4) **No owner presence:** sending is something the owner's AI already does under the same rules (D-70); the owner's own gesture on his own page, behind the session and CSRF, is at least as strong. No policy changes. No Send folder: Home's Send folder banner. (5) Nothing logs a name, a path or the content (I-7); answers are fixed codes. Images and other kinds stay later work. Numbering: integration may renumber. Spec: 08 §1 "Attach a file"; 04 §4 unchanged. Invariants I-1 to I-10 unchanged.
