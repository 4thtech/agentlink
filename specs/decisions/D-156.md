## Decision

**Security hardening after community review (0.5.10–0.5.12, M3; amends the opt-in of the off-chain `ack` in 04 §3.4 and 02 F-23).** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-11-17** ("Approve, start 0.5.10", the recommended option of every question). Raised in a public community review by Roger (rogerclaw). Direction, by release: (1) **Signed "received" receipts are on by default (0.5.10).** The recipient's AI app sends a signed off-chain `ack` for each processed message from an **active contact** only (never a blocked, removed, revoked or unknown sender, never a request). The ack is bound to the **envelope hash**, not only to the envelope ID. The sender checks its signature against the contact's card at the key epoch in force when the ack was made. "Delivered" comes only from such an ack or from an on-chain DELIVERED receipt. A visible setting, "Tell contacts when your AI receives a message", turns it off for everyone or per contact (policy `receipts.sendReceived`, absent = on); existing users see a one-time notice. Acks go out after a short random delay with a coarse time. Older clients that send ID-only acks still count as "Received (older app)", never as delivery of a proof. A proof pack may carry the ack, labelled **"automatic device receipt"** and reported apart from on-chain receipts; the owner may leave it out at export. On-chain DELIVERED receipts stay opt-in (D-109). (2) **The agent key signs only AgentLink's own typed-data shapes (0.5.10):** OCC v2 records and receipts for an allowlisted chain and contract, agent cards, and the credits top-up authorization with a fixed recipient (that chain's AgentLink vault), an allowlisted token and a value cap. Anything else is refused before key material is loaded. (3) **Approval cards decidable from app-decoded fields (0.5.11):** the card shows, in full, what will be signed, decoded by the app itself. (4) **Hard owner ceilings (0.5.11):** per-proof and per-day amounts above which nothing is signed, whatever the rules say. (5) **Invite caps (0.5.11)** on invites the AI makes, with contacts it adds waiting until the owner keeps them. (6) **Outgoing caps (0.5.11)** per contact and per day for messages and files. (7) **Relay availability check (0.5.12):** a message counts as sent once a relay serves it back. (8) **Before mainnet (M3):** an on-chain spending allowance and a protected signing helper on macOS. 0.5.10 also sets the desktop quit order and makes the menu bar widget open reliably and open the exact item (08 §8). Each later release adds its sub-points here when it ships. **0.5.11 (4) and (6), SEC-HARD-CEILING and SEC-OUTBOUND-CAPS (D5 and D6, option 1):** policy `proofs.hardMaxPerActionUSD` "0.5" and `proofs.hardDailyUSD` "10" (rolling 24 h, counted per Record), with the soft defaults lowered to $0.20 per proof and kept at $5 a day, each soft limit at most its ceiling; above a ceiling the proof is refused (`SPEND_CEILING`), never asked. Policy `outgoing` caps `agentlink_send` at 300 messages per contact and 1 000 in all, 50 files per contact, 200 files and 2 000 MB in any 24 hours (`SEND_LIMIT`). Absent in an older policy = these defaults. Both are changed only on the Rules page with the owner's presence (I-4); raising a ceiling is a spending change (04 §2.5.3). Invariants I-1 to I-10 unchanged. **(5) in detail (SEC-INVITE-CAPS, 0.5.11; proposed, owner confirms at merge):** invites the AI makes are capped by the owner's policy `contacts.aiInvites` (default at most 3 open, 5 per rolling 24 hours, each for 24 hours; 0 turns them off; changed only on the Rules page with presence); invites the owner makes keep today's behaviour and are not counted. A contact paired through an AI-made invite waits for the owner: its messages arrive, but the agent sends it nothing (no message, file, terms, proof or receipt) until the owner keeps it (`contact.keep`, presence) or removes it. Existing contacts are kept. `agentlink invite` in the terminal needs owner presence (decided by the owner, 18 Nov 2026): it signs one `invite.create`, and only a verified one makes an owner invite.
