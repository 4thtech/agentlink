# D-80: G-26: the STREAM header is authenticated

- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**G-26: the STREAM header is authenticated.** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-17 (option A).** In ALF1 (04 §4) each chunk's AES-256-GCM AAD is `header (17 B) ‖ u8(isFinal)`, where header is the exact 17 bytes `magic ‖ version ‖ chunkSize ‖ noncePrefix`, so a modified header or a missing final chunk fails decryption (STREAM construction). Header integrity no longer relies on `ciphertextSha256` alone. **Rules decided by Tali, 2026-10-18 (previously proposed):** the ALF1 version byte is `0x01` only; a plaintext of an exact multiple of `chunkSize` ends with a full final chunk, and an empty final chunk exists only for the empty file; `chunkSize` 1 B to 16 MiB; at most 2^32 chunks. Unblocks M1-1d and the M1-1i STREAM vectors.
