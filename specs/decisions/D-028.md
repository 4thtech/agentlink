## Decision

Hosting on Cloudflare Workers (zone `4thtech.io` is on Cloudflare). Testing/beta hostnames under `agentlink.4thtech.io` (separate origins per 08 §2.3, e.g. `console.`, `verify.`, `invite.`, `pay.`, `submit.agentlink.4thtech.io`); staging previews on `workers.dev`. The production marketing domain (not yet chosen or registered; e.g. a `.xyz`-style name with `app.` for the console) is decided later, depending on availability. After the move, `agentlink.4thtech.io` stays as the permanent testing environment
