# D-25: Continuous delivery: merging to `main` deploys automatically to staging/testnet with smoke tests

- **Status:** Decided (12 Oct 2026); amended by D-51 and D-52
- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Specs:** none
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

Continuous delivery: merging to `main` deploys automatically to staging/testnet with smoke tests; promotion to production (npm `latest`, ~~production Workers and sites~~) waits for one approval click by the owner on the GitHub deployment (superseded by D-52 for Workers and sites, which deploy automatically once staging and smoke tests are green; still in force for npm `latest`). Mainnet contract deploys, contract upgrades, Safe operations and production secrets stay manual and owner-only. `DEPLOY_FREEZE` stops all deploys
