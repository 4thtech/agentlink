## Decision

**G-25: `agentlink_verify_proof_pack` `path` is confined to two folders.** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-18 (option a).** `path` may point only inside `proofs.exportFolder` or `files.inboxFolder`: lexical check before any disk access, then realpath, no-follow open, regular file, 256 KiB cap, device and inode match; fixed error codes (`PATH_NOT_ALLOWED`, `PACK_TOO_LARGE`, `PACK_INVALID`) with no echo of bytes or paths. The pack is untrusted (I-5) and the verifier reads only the known Notary of a supported chain. Keeps 06 §1 rule 4 and I-4 intact. Spec in 06 §2.4 and §9.
