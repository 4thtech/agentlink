# D-157: The message network view: server hosts under the hood, own list of 2 to 8

- **Status:** Decided (owner, 19 Nov 2026: hosts shown under the hood only, own list 2 to 8, 30-day overlap, sign-in-to-read servers allowed with a warning); mechanics proposed, owner confirms at merge
- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Specs:** 07, 08
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**A live view of the message network under the hood, and the own-list bound (0.5.14, cards NET-PROBE, NET-STATS, NET-ROUTE, NET-UI-A; amends 08 §1 "counts only").** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-11-19.** (1) **Server hosts are shown, under the hood only.** Advanced › Message network (Under the hood) may show each server's host: the origin host only, never the scheme, a path, a query or credentials, and a port only when it is not 443. A contact's server hosts are shown only when the owner expands that section; they are inbound content (I-5): plain text, length-capped, never a link, markup or a NIP-11 field. Outside Under the hood and Advanced the app still shows counts only; a person who never opens Under the hood sees no change. (2) **The own list holds 2 to 8 servers**, as 07 §4.3 "Counts" says; the code that allowed 16 for the agent's own list is fixed (`MAX_OWN_RELAYS`); a list received with a contact's card keeps its bound of 16. (3) **After a server change the previous list stays readable for 30 days** (card M1-7j, release 0.5.15; noted here, not built in 0.5.14). (4) **Servers that require a sign-in to read** (NIP-42 auth-required) will be allowed in a custom list with a warning when at least 2 other servers are ready (0.5.15); in 0.5.14 the view only reports the flag. Mechanics (proposed): a bounded per-server probe of the own and legacy servers only, while the view is open (07 §4.3 "Message network view"); per-server counters in `relay_stats` (07 §3.2), counters only; `GET /api/ui/network` and the live `network` event (08 §1).
