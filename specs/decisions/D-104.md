## Decision

**M1-4d-c: owner-approval and receipt-link rules for the proof-pack verifier.** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-19:** "write the owner-approval verifier rules into 07 §8". A signed `ownerApproval` is checked in order, fail closed: the BIP-340 signature under the owner-action tag (04 §2.5.1); `actionDigest` = sha256(JCS(action)); the scope's `termsHash` is the hash of the stored terms (04 §3.4); the scope is bound to this record (04 §3.4.2); the owner key agrees with the signer's card when the pack carries one. Any failure is the fatal code `owner_approval_invalid`. A signer-side approval without opened content is reported `recordBound: false`, never as approving this record. A standing rule is reported with its `policyHash`, not checked. A receipt's `txHash` is confirmed only by the Notary's `ReceiptRecorded` event at the configured Notary address for this record, type, signer and time; not found or a read failure is `not_checked`. User wording (11 §5): "The owner approval in the proof is not valid for this record." Spec: 07 §8.
