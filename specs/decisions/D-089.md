# D-89: G-17: the EIP-3009 flag is off-chain, and the vault checks the received amount

- **Status:** Decided (owner, 19 Oct 2026)
- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Specs:** 05
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**G-17: the EIP-3009 flag is off-chain, and the vault checks the received amount.** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-19 (option a).** The per-token flag lives in the per-chain config, read by the client and the submitter. The vault keeps no flag; `depositWithAuthorization` requires an allowed token and `value ≥ minDeposit`, measures its balance before and after `receiveWithAuthorization`, and reverts `DepositMismatch()` unless the increase equals `value`. Spec in 05 §2.5 and §7; the contract work is M1-3c, in the private contracts repository.
