## Decision

**Local history management: Archive and Delete on this computer, Free up space, tombstones, a saved-files index, owner-signed retention (`history.*`), and the AI proposes while the owner approves (retention plan RET; amends D-134 (2) "history kept as today", the 04 §4.1 sentence "AgentLink never deletes the person's files" and the 06 §2.0 "No tool is destructive" line).** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-11-12:** owner decisions OD-1 to OD-6 of the retention plan, all taken as recommended. (1) **OD-1 Inbox files:** AgentLink deletes only a file it saved itself and that is still unchanged (same device and inode, size and hash, under the 04 §4.1 directory-handle rules), only when the owner asks or under the owner's own retention setting, and only by moving it to the OS Trash; a moved, renamed or edited file is never touched, and where no Trash is possible the owner is asked before a permanent delete. (2) **OD-2 Defaults:** messages are kept forever by default (`history.messagesKeepDays: 0`); saved-file retention is off for existing installs (a stored policy without `history` reads as `0`, plus a one-time "Free up space" hint) and 90 days for policies first created from 0.5.2 on (`history.filesKeepDays: 90`, files go to the Trash). (3) **OD-3 AI:** the AI proposes, the owner approves: a later tool `agentlink_tidy` (RET-7, 0.5.2 or later; its own tool-surface spec PR and Fable review) takes only a filter and always answers `pending_approval`, and the owner approves the exact list in the app with presence. No MCP tool deletes directly or sets `history.*` (I-4). **No MCP change in 0.5.1:** the AI only points to Settings › Storage. (4) **OD-4 Notarized messages:** a delete removes the body and saved files and keeps the ciphertext envelope, the proof and its receipts, so the proof stays verifiable and a pack can still be exported without readable content. (5) **OD-5 Presence:** one presence gesture per delete sheet (Touch ID, Windows Hello, passkey or the master code), then a 10-second Undo before the core commits. (6) **OD-6 Scope:** 0.5.1 ships only the read-only Storage view (RET-1) and this spec (RET-0); every destructive part ships in 0.5.2, each with a Fable audit. *Proposed, owner confirms at merge (RET-0):* the words (Archive hides and deletes nothing; Delete removes from this computer only, and the contact is not told: "Only on this computer; {Name} keeps their copy and is not told."); nothing is sent to the contact, a relay, the drop or any 4thTech service (zero access); a deleted envelope stays as a tombstone so the duplicate check drops relay replays; a `saved_files` index that survives the day-7 `files` shred; protected items never deleted (pairing handshakes and acks, `card-update`, items that approvals, commitments, the budget ledger or the outbox need, open terms); I-9 read as a capability that an owner's own deliberate delete does not breach; a delete removes items from future kits only and the UI says older backups may still contain them. **Threat note (author):** no new authority for the AI or any contact; the delete is an owner operation with presence or an owner-signed policy, fail closed when the policy is invalid or the ID paused; a same-user shell could already delete the store or the Inbox (G-1).
