# D-84: Streaming download verification: quarantine instead of pre-check

- **Status:** Decided (owner, 18 Oct 2026)
- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Specs:** 04, 07
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**Streaming download verification: quarantine instead of pre-check.** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-18.** 04 §4 Integrity said the recipient checks `ciphertextSha256` before decrypting. A streaming download (07 §5.2, M1-6b) cannot hash the whole object before the first chunk decrypts without buffering it, so the rule now reads: the recipient verifies `ciphertextSha256` before any plaintext is released or committed, and `plaintextSha256` after. A streaming recipient may decrypt into a private quarantine (a new hidden temporary file, created exclusively without following links, mode 0600, in the target folder) and renames it into place only after the final chunk and both hashes verify; it removes it on any failure, and removes stale quarantine files at start-up. Every ALF1 chunk is authenticated under `fileKey` with the header and `isFinal` in the AAD (D-80), so no third party can place plaintext in the quarantine, and G-46's same-bytes-or-failure guarantee holds because nothing is accepted before both hashes pass. Residual: unverified, sender-authored plaintext sits in the hidden temporary file until the end. Raised in IR-M1-6b-2-1 M-2.
