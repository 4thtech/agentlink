# D-46: Stablecoin-only fees at launch

- **Status:** Decided (12 Oct 2026)
- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Specs:** none
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**Stablecoin-only fees at launch:** USDC, USDT where available, and xDAI (the native coin on Gnosis, USD-pegged). The fee is then exactly the USD price, with no oracle and no repricing. Native volatile coins (e.g. ETH on Base) come later, behind a bounded price-updater role that needs a contract design and security review. **Amends D-04**
