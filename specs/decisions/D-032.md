# D-32: Negotiate-and-agree is the flagship journey (J8)

- **Status:** Decided (12 Oct 2026)
- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Specs:** none
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**Negotiate-and-agree is the flagship journey (J8).** Agents negotiate free over Talk using a structured `terms` schema (parties, deliverables, price, deadlines, validity). The final terms are notarized, and the counterparty's ACCEPTED receipt makes the proof pack a **proof of agreement** (mutual assent), not just a timestamp. Typed offer/accept/reject messages with `terms` become P0
