# D-158: Use your own servers: a list of 2 to 8, a tested list, a 30-day overlap per leaving server and an announce opt-out

- **Status:** Decided (owner, 19–20 Nov 2026: scope, list size and mix-in, test outcomes, public servers only, overlap rule, owner operation, announce opt-out, design and label); mechanics proposed, owner confirms at merge
- **Date:** 2026-11-20
- **Specs:** 04, 07, 08, 11
- **Index:** [all decisions](README.md)

## Decision

**"Use your own servers", end to end (0.5.15, cards M1-7j, NET-PRECHECK, NET-OWNER-SET, NET-CLI, NET-UI-B, NET-ANNOUNCE-OPTOUT, NET-HOSTILE, NET-BAR24; amends D-157 (3) and (4) and 07 §4.3 "No previous list is kept").** **Decided by Tali in chat, 19 and 20 Nov 2026.** (1) **Scope.** 0.5.15 completes choosing your own message servers end to end: the app (Advanced › Message network, Under the hood), the terminal (`agentlink owner servers`), the server test, the overlap, the announce opt-out and the docs. Nothing changes by default; a person who never opens Under the hood sees no change. (2) **List size and mix-in.** A list holds 2 to 8 servers. By default AgentLink mixes in 2 of its automatic servers, and they count toward the 8, so the owner adds up to 6 and at least 2 of the owner's own must be ready. The mix-in can be switched off, with a warning; the list is then 2 to 8 of the owner's own, at least 2 ready. (3) **Test outcomes.** Each server is tested on this computer before it can be used: **ready**; **slow** (a round trip over 1 s; counts as ready, with a warning); **needs sign-in** (to read; allowed with a warning only when at least 2 other servers of the list are ready); **refused** (anything else: it does not accept a message from someone it does not know, does not give the test message back, cannot be reached, or is not allowed). (4) **Public `wss://` servers only.** No private, loopback or link-local address and no IP literal; the relay URL rule and the connect-time address check of 04 §4 are unchanged. An option for private-network servers was assessed and rejected on 19 Nov. (5) **Overlap.** Every server that leaves the list stays readable for 30 days from that change, each with its own end; there is no wait between changes, and "Use AgentLink's automatic servers" (reset) is allowed at any time. At most 16 servers are kept readable this way; a change that would need more is refused with the date from which it fits. (6) **Owner operation.** A list change, a reset and the opt-out are owner operations with presence and an owner signature (Touch ID, Windows Hello, a passkey or the master code); there is no MCP or AI path (I-4). (7) **Announce opt-out, in 0.5.15.** "Don't also announce on the default servers" is offered only with the mix-in off. When on, the ID's owner card-update goes to its own and leaving servers only, and the card-update fan-out to the old default servers stops too: the owner chose this on 20 Nov, accepting that a contact still listening only on the old servers may miss the update; the warning says so. Restoring from a backup keeps the list and the opt-out. **Fully quiet (decided by Tali in chat, 20 Nov 2026):** while the opt-out is on, the ID also reads, subscribes to and probes no default or old default server, and publishes nothing to a leaving server that is one, except a server a contact's own card lists, for that contact; its own previous servers stay readable for their 30 days; the warning says the old public servers stop at once and that contacts still using an old copy of the contact details may not reach the ID until they receive the update. **Note (mechanics, owner confirms at merge):** with a list change the announce choice is carried inside that change's owner-signed operation and stored with the card; `network.announce` is the separate signed action only when the list does not change. (8) **Design.** The C1–C8 wireframes (C7b included) are the design, styled from existing components. The network diagram labels this computer "{name} · this computer". (9) **Out of scope:** automatic swapping of servers, private or home-network servers and IP literals, and custom servers for a cloud agent (M2-2e). Mechanics (proposed): 07 §3 and §4.3 (the `prev` list, the overlap rule, the pre-check, the opt-out), 04 §2.5, §2.6, §4 and §7, 08 §1 (`POST /api/ui/network/test`, `POST /api/ui/network/servers`, `POST /api/ui/network/reset`, `POST /api/ui/network/announce`, `GET /api/ui/network/progress`), 11 §5.14 (strings).
