## Decision

**Contract and envelope decisions of 14 Oct 2026 (amends D-59).** (1) **Batches (G-4):** `notarizeBatch` and `acknowledgeBatch` are all-or-nothing, except that an item already on-chain in identical form is skipped with no charge and a `RecordSkipped`/`ReceiptSkipped` event; any other failure reverts the batch; single calls still revert (05 §3.3). (2) **Gas:** `notarize` (credits, EOA) ≤ 170k (05 §3.4). (3) **Pause scope:** pause also blocks receipts (`acknowledge`, `acknowledgeBatch`), as well as charges, deposits, notarization and registration; it still never blocks `withdraw`, `withdrawIntegrator`, `sweep` or the Registry `revoke`. This amends D-59 item (6) and G-39 (05 §5.1). (4) **Fresh salt per Record:** 32 CSPRNG bytes, never reused or derived (04 §5). (5) **One plaintext for every recipient (G-46)** is a requirement (04 §3.3); the construction is open, and its timing was accepted in D-62.
