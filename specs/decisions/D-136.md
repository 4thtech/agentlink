## Decision

**Business agents: companies connect the agents they already run, through a business agent in their own Cloudflare account.** **Decided by Tali in chat, 2026-10-28** (Decision 20: decisions 1 to 10 and 12 accepted; pilot: to be named by the owner; proposal `project/process/proposals/BUSINESS-AGENTS.md`). Companies connect the agents they already run to AgentLink through a business agent: the D-131 cloud agent in the company's own Cloudflare account, in business mode. People add it through a multi-use business link that requires a verified domain (04 §2.7), negotiate with typed terms, approve with owner presence, and get a proof the company pays for. The company's agent uses the AgentLink tools through the business agent's remote MCP server and is woken by an invoker (Claude, then OpenAI, A2A, or a signed webhook) with a fixed wake-up text that carries only an opaque local contact ID and a token scoped to one conversation; inbound content, including contact names, never enters its instructions. Invoker grants follow D-131 (m) (at most 30 days). Auto-agreed commitments (accepts and firm offers) are sell-only, hard-capped and always recorded with proof. A business contact whose domain check lapses is paused, never downgraded to self-declared. 4thTech hosts nothing that holds a key (D-37). No payments between agents; payment stays on the company's checkout. Companies pay a Business plan for operated services; users stay free. 0.5.0 carries it behind a pilot switch; general availability in 0.5.x. Invariants I-1 to I-10 unchanged. Spec: BUSINESS-AGENTS; 04 §2.7; 08 §3.
