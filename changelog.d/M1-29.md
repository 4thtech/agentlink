- `agentlink init --hook` adds an optional start-of-session note to Claude Code: a line or two with unread counts, requests and contact names from `agentlink status --brief`, never message text. It is off until you ask for it, shows the exact change to `~/.claude/settings.json` first, writes it only after you confirm, and runs the installed program by its full path, never `npx`. Codex, Gemini CLI, OpenCode and Antigravity are documented as not available (M1-29, S1, 06 §7, D-53).
