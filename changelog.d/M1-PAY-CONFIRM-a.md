---
section: Security
---

- Paid actions need your own verification (M1-PAY-CONFIRM-a, S2, D-132, 04 §2.5.3): approving a proof above your limits, or changing those limits, now goes through a confirmation that asks your computer to verify it is you (Touch ID, Windows Hello, a passkey or a PIN) and is bound to the amount and purpose shown. It works once, for 2 minutes, and only on your own page; your AI can't confirm it for you. The backup code still works, with three tries. Proofs inside your limits stay automatic. The confirmation sheet itself arrives with M1-PAY-CONFIRM-b.
