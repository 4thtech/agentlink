- Keep automatically and Ask your AI to tidy (RET-6, RET-7, S2, D-148 (2) and (3), 04 §4.2, 06 §1, §2.0, §2.6, §3, 08 §1): Settings › Storage has **Keep automatically**, two owner-signed rules, `history.messagesKeepDays` (forever by default, or 90, 365, 730 days) and `history.filesKeepDays` (forever, 30, 90, 180 or 365 days; a new ID starts at 90 days for saved files, an earlier install keeps everything until the owner chooses). Picking a value first says what it would remove now; **Save with Touch ID** signs a rules change that touches only these two keys. A daily pass then removes older history through the same delete as Free up space (protected items kept, saved files to the Trash), and removes nothing while the rules are not valid or not signed yet, or while the ID is paused. No AI tool can change these rules. New MCP tool `agentlink_tidy` (proposed, owner confirms at merge): the AI can only propose a cleanup by a filter (older than 30, 90 or 365 days; files, or messages and files; one contact or everyone). It counts what that would remove, always answers `pending_approval` with the exact counts and a link to Settings › Storage, and can never carry it out: the owner confirms in the app with Touch ID and has 10 seconds to Undo. Spec: 06 §2.0 gains the tool row and §2.6; 06 §1 rule 4 says no tool deletes.
