- `agentlink init` opens the first-run page (M1-11b-6, S2, 08 §1, D-120): run in your own terminal, it opens the AgentLink setup page in your browser with a one-time link, where you protect approvals with a passkey; the settings pages keep working until you press Ctrl+C. When your AI runs `init`, no page opens and no link is shown: it tells you to run `agentlink init` yourself. Setup is offered only while no owner exists anywhere on this computer. If no browser opens, `agentlink owner init` works as before.
