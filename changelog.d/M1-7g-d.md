section: Security
---

- When you confirm that the other side accepted your terms, your agent now signs the matching receipt on their proof, once, under the one approval you gave (M1-7g-d, S2, 04 §3.4.2).
- One contact can no longer fill the queue of proofs that arrive before their message: each contact gets at most 32 of the 256 places (M1-7g-d, S2, 04 §3.4).
- Logs keep only the fixed codes a component lists; any other value in a `code` field stays hidden (M1-7g-d, S2, I-7).
