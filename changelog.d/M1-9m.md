- `agentlink policy` (M1-9m, S2, 06 §8, 04 §4): in a terminal you opened yourself, `agentlink policy allow-folder <path>` and `agentlink policy remove-folder <path>` choose the folders your agent may send files from, each signed with your owner code; `agentlink policy show` lists them with a few other rules. AgentLink stores the folder's real path and refuses links, relative paths, your whole computer or home folder, and hidden or private folders. Your AI can't change these rules.
