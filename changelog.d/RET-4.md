- Delete history and Archive in the app (RET-4, RET-5, S2, 04 §4.2, 08 §1, 11 §5, D-148): a person's menu offers **Archive** (no Touch ID; nothing is deleted and your AI still sees the conversation; the app keeps the choice for every browser) and **Delete history…** (messages and files, messages only or files only, any age or older than 30, 90 or 365 days); a conversation has **Select**, then "Delete {n} messages"; Settings › Storage has **Free up space** (by person, by age, files only, then **Review**) and **Show in Finder** for the Inbox folder. Every delete first shows what it removes and what is always kept (pairing, agreed terms, approvals, receipts), asks for Touch ID, Windows Hello, a passkey or the master code once, and then waits 10 seconds with **Undo** before anything is removed; afterwards it says "Moved {n} files to the Trash." (on Windows saved files are not moved yet: remove them yourself). The server fixes the list when it is shown and applies only that list; a list that changed in between is refused. Only on this computer: your contacts keep their copies and are not told. No AI tool can reach any of this. Core: a person and an age together are one delete selector.
