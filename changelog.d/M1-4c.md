- Proofs are sent through a list of submitters with failover, or directly from the agent's own account when no submitter gets them on-chain (M1-4c, S2, 07 §6.2, §7; 04 §5, §7). Every result is read from the chain, never taken from a submitter's answer: a wrong transaction hash, a silent drop, a modified record or a false failure is noticed and the same signed item goes to the next route. A replacement is allowed only when the earlier item can never land (04 §5). Deposits read the current allowance first and reset it to 0 for tokens that need it; after a deposit or a refusal for missing credits the chain is selected again from fresh reads.
