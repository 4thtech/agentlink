- Recorded counter-offers (M1-9d-5, S2, 06 §2.2a, 04 §3.4.2; J4, J8): `agentlink_respond_to_terms` `counter` with `notarize` sends the new version as a firm offer and records it, as `agentlink_propose_terms` with `notarize` does. The owner approves once, and that one approval covers the new terms and the proof fee. With no credits, nothing is sent and the owner is not asked; a newer version from the contact before it is sent voids the approval. The other side's one approval of an accept adds the Accepted receipt. A recorded rejection is still not available.
