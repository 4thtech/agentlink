### Added

- When your AI needs your approval, AgentLink now opens the approval page in your browser and the AI shows you its link (M1-11a-b, S2, 08 §1, 06 §2.5): approve or deny there with your owner code or passkey, or run `agentlink approve <id>` in your own terminal as before. The link the AI sees opens nothing by itself, and your AI can't approve for you.

### Changed

- The release bundle check allows `node:http` (the local approval page) and `node:child_process` only as `execFile` (opening your browser) (M1-11a-b, D-76).

### Security

- The local approval page opens your browser and shows notifications only through programs at fixed system paths, never ones found through your PATH, and alerts you at most once every 30 seconds however many approvals are waiting (M1-11a-b, IR-M1-11a-b-1).
