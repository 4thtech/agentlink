section: Changed

- Message network (Under the hood) stays compact with many servers: each server is one line (dot, host, state and time) that opens to its details, with the small 24-hour accepted-versus-failed bar back inside it. Your own servers show the first 5, then "Show all N servers"; old read-only servers sit in their own folded group; a server that is slow, down or blocked is always on top and never folded. The diagram draws at most 5 servers and 5 contacts, then "+N more", which opens the full list below (NET-COMPACT, NET-BAR24, S1, D-116, 11 §5).
- Your servers panel: long lists scroll inside the panel, and the sign-in warning reads "That's fine as long as 2 other servers work, and they do." (NET-COMPACT, S1, 11 §5.14).
