- Back up in the app now saves every ID you have on this computer into one recovery kit, with one master code entry (UX1113-R2b, S2, D-147, 04 §2.3.3, §2.5): the app opens your other IDs one at a time during that one save, never two at once, and closes each again; an ID kept with a passphrase file is not included and is named so you can back it up in the terminal. The Backup page reads the record of your last kit, "Show in Finder" finds that kit, and "Check that my kit opens" works right after a save.
