---
section: Changed
---

- Proof checks are stricter and say more (M1-4d-c, S2, 07 §8; D-96, D-97): a record whose receipt flags disagree with its receipt times is refused as unreadable; a signer account that no longer confirms a record is reported ("The signer's account no longer confirms this record. The proof network checked it when the record was made.") without failing the proof; each receipt's link is checked or marked as not checked; and the owner approval a proof carries is checked against the record, or reported as a standing rule.
