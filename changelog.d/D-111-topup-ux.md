- Direction for adding credits (D-111, docs only): one link from the agent opens the pay page, crypto first: use crypto you already have, or send from an exchange to the agent's address with a QR code, with amounts in dollars and no network choice on the default path. The agent explains the one step, watches for the funds and confirms the credits, and never asks for keys or moves funds. Users pay every cost; 4thTech subsidises none (the testnet faucet excepted). Pay by card comes last, after the legal entity is decided. Design brief `docs/design/topup-ux.md`; spec lines in 06 §2.4, 08 §1 and §2.3, 11 §5.1 and §6.7, 12 §1 and §4.5; new cards M1-26-e, M1-34, M2-18a–d and M3-4c. The card approach and provider, the top-up link format and who pays for receipts (user-paid in any case) are open owner decisions.
