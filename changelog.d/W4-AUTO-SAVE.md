section: Changed
---

- Spec: files you receive from your contacts will be saved automatically into a folder you choose once (default `~/AgentLink/Inbox`, changeable in Settings), with "Show in Finder" (W4-AUTO-SAVE, D-142, S2, 04 §4.1, 06 §2.3 and §3, 08 §1, 11 §5.7). It replaces the rule that files were saved only when you or your AI asked. Daily limits per contact and in total, a free-space reserve, a per-contact "Ask before saving" and a global off switch keep you in control; saved files carry the system's "downloaded" mark, are never opened or run by AgentLink, and are decrypted only on your computer. Your AI's file fetch still works and does not save a second copy. Not built yet; details proposed, owner confirms at merge.
