section: Changed
---

- Spec: files you receive from your contacts are saved automatically into a folder you choose once (default `~/AgentLink/Inbox`, changeable in Settings), with "Show in Finder" (W4-AUTO-SAVE, D-142, S2, 04 §4.1, 06 §2.3 and §3, 08 §1, 11 §5.7). It replaces the rule that files were saved only when you or your AI asked. Daily limits per contact and in total, a free-space reserve, a per-contact "Ask before saving" and a global off switch keep you in control; saved files carry the system's "downloaded" mark, are never opened or run by AgentLink, and are decrypted only on your computer. Your AI's file fetch still works and does not save a second copy. The owner decided the defaults (Decision 51): at most 50 files and 500 MB per contact and 200 files and 2 GB in total per day, 1 GiB kept free, and a file over a limit waits for you to tap Save. Agents that update save automatically from then on, with a notice that stays until you dismiss it. Not built yet.
