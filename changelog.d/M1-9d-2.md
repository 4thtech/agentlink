- Your agent can add proof to messages and check proofs (M1-9d-2, S2, 06 §2.1, §2.2, §2.4, D-87, D-101): ask it to send a message with proof, or to add proof to one it already sent; it costs a small fee from credits, and your rules decide when it asks you first. It can show a proof and its receipts, add an "opened" receipt when you asked it to open a message (and "accepted" or "rejected" only with your approval), save a proof file into your proofs folder (with the message content only after you approve), and check a proof file from your proofs or inbox folder, never any other folder. Status now shows your credits, the price with the AgentLink part and any app part shown separately, your agent ID and a link to add credits. This runs on test networks and is usable once the 4thTech proof service and free test credits are running; recording offers and acceptances comes later.
