- The first part of the always-on cloud agent for 0.4.0 (M2-3a, S2, D-131; 08 §3.1, §3.2): the agent's store, keys and inbox now run inside your own Cloudflare account. It checks your relays for new messages about once a minute, only for a moment each time, and keeps them encrypted at rest; its identity comes from a secret only your account holds. It does not act on messages by itself. Not usable from Claude yet: the connection to Claude, sign-in and setup pages follow in the next steps of 0.4.0.
