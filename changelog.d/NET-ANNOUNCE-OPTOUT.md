- "Don't also announce on the default servers" (NET-ANNOUNCE-OPTOUT, S2, D-158 (7), 04 §2.5.1, 07 §4.3, 08 §1): for a company that runs everything on its own servers. Under Advanced › Message network, with your own servers and AgentLink's automatic ones switched off, one switch keeps this ID off the default servers entirely: its own update goes only to your servers, and the old update fan-out to contacts stops. It can be turned on or off without changing your servers, with one Touch ID, passkey or master code (the system sheet says what you approve). The warning says what it costs: another computer finds the ID only with your owner link or a backup, and contacts still only on AgentLink's old servers may miss your new details. Your contacts still get your messages on their own servers, and new invites carry your own list. Going back to automatic servers clears it; restoring from a backup keeps it. AI apps cannot reach it.
