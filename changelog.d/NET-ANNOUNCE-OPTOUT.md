- "Don't also announce on the default servers" (NET-ANNOUNCE-OPTOUT, S2, D-158 (7), 04 §2.5.1, 07 §4.3, 08 §1): for a company that runs everything on its own servers. Under Advanced › Message network, with your own servers and AgentLink's automatic ones switched off, one switch keeps this ID off the default servers entirely: nothing is sent to, read from or checked on AgentLink's public servers, old or current, from that moment (also when you just moved off them); your own previous servers stay readable for 30 days. It can be turned on or off without changing your servers, with one Touch ID, passkey or master code (the system sheet says what you approve). The warning says what it costs: another computer finds the ID only with your owner link or a backup, and contacts still using an old copy of your contact details may not reach you until they receive the update. Your contacts still get your messages on their own servers, and new invites carry your own list. Going back to automatic servers clears it; restoring from a backup keeps it. AI apps cannot reach it.
