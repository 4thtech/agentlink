- The local web app has its Contacts page (M1-11c-a2, S2, D-118): your contacts by name, with their label. For a new contact it shows the 12-digit safety code with a short nudge to compare it by phone, and "They match", "They don't match" and "Skip". "They match" marks the contact Safety code checked after you confirm with your passkey or owner code; "They don't match" explains what that can mean and offers to remove the contact. You can also rename or remove a contact. With no contacts yet, it suggests asking your AI to create an invite.
