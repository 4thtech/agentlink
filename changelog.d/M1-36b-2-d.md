### Security

- A folder allowed for sending is pinned to where it really was when you allowed it (M1-36b-2-d, S2, 04 §4, IR-M1-36b-2-c-1 L3). If that folder, or a folder above it, is later replaced by a link, AgentLink no longer uses it for sending, so a link can't widen what your AI may send. To use it again, allow the real folder again in your own terminal.
- `policy allow-send-folder` checks the Send folder again after you type your owner code, just before your rules change (M1-36b-2-d, L1). If it was renamed, replaced, turned into a link or given files in the meantime, nothing changes.

### Fixed

- `policy allow-send-folder` now sees `~/AgentLink/Send` in your rules as already allowed and doesn't ask for your owner code again (M1-36b-2-d, L2).
