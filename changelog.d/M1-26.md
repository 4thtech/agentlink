### Added

- Automatic choice of where a proof is paid, in development and not user-facing yet (M1-26, S1, 07 §6.3, D-40): the client checks the agent's credits on every supported network at once, uses the default network when it has enough credits, otherwise the cheapest one that does, and otherwise says credits are needed and where adding them costs least. A network that does not answer in time is skipped, never chosen. Only test networks are ever used in this release.

### Changed

- 07 §6.3 describes the selection order, the tie-breaks, the "funded" rule, the per-network time budget and cache, and the mainnet guard (M1-26; proposed, the owner confirms at merge). The technical docs' SDK packages page is updated.
