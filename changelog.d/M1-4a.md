### Added

- The chain client foundation, in development and not user-facing yet (M1-4a, S2, 07 §6.1, §6.2): the client knows Base Sepolia from the shipped entry and the pinned contracts release, picks the chain on its own, reads through several public endpoints with timeouts, retries and failover, and builds proof records and receipts that match the shared test vectors exactly. Every record gets a fresh random salt and nonce the caller cannot choose, and signing goes only through the agent's key store.

### Changed

- 07 §6.1 and §6.2 describe the chain registry, the RPC URL rule, the read-only client and the Record builder's salt and nonce rules (M1-4a; proposed, the owner confirms at merge). The technical docs' SDK packages page has a new "Chain client" section, marked in development (M1-4a).

### Security

- RPC endpoints, shipped or added by the user, are treated as secrets and never logged or returned: every read-client error is a fixed-code `ChainError` with no address, path or query (M1-4a, IR-M1-4a-1 M-2, I-7). An owner's own endpoint may carry its provider key in the path. Endpoints must be `https://` with a public host name (no IP address, no local or special-use name), with no user info, query or fragment; in Node, private and loopback addresses are refused at resolve time and the checked address is pinned for each request (`nodeRpcFetch`, 04 §4, IR-M1-4a-1 M-1). Requests never follow redirects, and only read methods are sent. The client can neither type nor encode an admin, upgrade or module call, and offers no EIP-3009 deposit until the contracts accept it (AUDIT-FABLE-OCC19-1 M-1, L-1).
