- A small calm mark on a conversation or file that is moving right now (U3-map-2, S1; b11 §32; owner decision 8). In Messages, a row shows "Sending" or "Receiving" with a short arrow while a message or file moves for that contact; in Files, the row of a file being sent or received shows it. It lasts about a minute, then settles. With reduced motion the arrow stays still and the word "now" shows. It shows a word and a direction only, never content, and there is no hover preview. The Home mini map, the Contacts map and the compact strip follow in 0.4.5.
