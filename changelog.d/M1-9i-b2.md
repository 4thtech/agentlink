- The binding between your own storage credentials and the owner's signed connect (M1-9i-b2, S2, 04 §2.5.1, D-115): a salted digest of the access key and secret, so a changed or swapped credential stops own storage instead of being used, and the owner-only building blocks for backup export, restore and storage credentials, which no agent tool can reach. The `storage connect`, `backup export` and `backup restore` commands that use them come in later cards.
