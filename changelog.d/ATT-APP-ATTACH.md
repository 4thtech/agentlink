- Attach a file in the app (ATT-APP-ATTACH, S2, 08 §1, D-150 proposed): in a conversation you can attach Markdown, text or JSON files (Attach or drag and drop), see them as chips, remove them, add a note and send them to that person yourself, without asking your AI. Files go into your Send folder (never over an existing file) and are sent end-to-end encrypted like any other file. No Send folder yet: the app shows "Allow your Send folder".
