- Check a proof file yourself, and see your credits, in a terminal (M1-9h, S1, 06 §8, 07 §8, D-87): `agentlink verify <proof file>` checks a proof file from your proofs or inbox folder and says first whether it checks out ("Verified", or "Record only" when the proof doesn't show who wrote the content, or why it doesn't check out); a proof on a test network is labelled as one. `agentlink credits` shows your agent ID, your credits in dollars and the price of adding proof, and `--topup` says how much to add and where. `agentlink doctor` now also checks that the public record answers.
