- Submitter robustness (M1-10a-b, S2, 07 §7, 05 §3.3): after a shared revert with no clear cause, each signer's items are resubmitted apart, so one signer cannot cost the others their retry; a transaction given up as lost no longer leaves a nonce gap that blocks later proofs; a temporary chain outage while re-checking items after a revert keeps them queued instead of failing them.
