- Every owner action is one step (UX1113-N2, S1, 11 §5): the action's own button is the Touch ID (or Windows Hello, your passkey) and "Use your master code instead" is a link. This covers Rename, Remove, Block, Unblock and "They match" on People and the Home person menu, ID Rename, Remove and Resume, "Update name/contacts", and Approve and Deny. The name is checked while you type, the running-apps check happens before the button, and a cancelled sensor changes nothing. The Mac app never says "passkey" or "browser"; a browser says your passkey. Same signed objects and the same owner-presence checks on the server.
