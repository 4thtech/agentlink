section: Security
---

- Limits on invites your AI makes (SEC-INVITE-CAPS, S2, D-156): your AI can have at most 3 invites open and make at most 5 a day, and each one works for 24 hours. Change these in Settings › Rules › More rules ("Invites your AI makes"; 0 turns them off). Invites you make in People work as before. Someone who connects through an invite your AI made shows as "New contact via your AI" on People, Home and in the menu bar widget: their messages arrive, but your AI can't send them anything (no messages, files, terms or proofs) until you tap Keep. Contacts you already have are not affected.
