- Home is the app's first screen. Anything waiting for your approval is a card on top (Approve or Deny with Touch ID; "+2 more" when several wait). Below it: Invite someone, and the people your AI talks to, each with their last activity. Open a person to see the conversation both ways (each message you sent says Sent, Waiting to send or Not delivered; Copy on every message; earlier messages load on request), the files you exchanged (Save to Inbox, Show in Finder, Show in Explorer on Windows, Show in folder on Linux) and a menu to rename them, check the safety code or remove them. With Under the hood on, the same screen becomes a table with search and an agent filter, each message shows its ID, exact time, size, agent, receipt and proof, and several free requests can be approved together; anything that costs money or commits you is still approved one at a time (W4-HOME, Decisions 47–50).
