section: Security
---

- Testnet faucet robustness (M1-10b-c, S2, 05 §9): a faucet top-up stuck waiting to be confirmed no longer blocks every later claim; such claims get the usual "try again later" answer and nothing is sent. Every faucet gas limit is capped, so a bad simulation result cannot spend the faucet's testnet funds. The submitter runbook explains what rolling the faucet back to an older version does to its claim records.
