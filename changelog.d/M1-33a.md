---
section: Changed
---

- Delivery receipts are now off by default (M1-33a, S2, D-109, 06 §3): a delivery receipt shows publicly that a recorded message reached your agent, so your agent no longer signs one on its own unless you turn it on, for everyone or for one contact. If your earlier rules had them on from the old default, `agentlink doctor` says so, and the next time you sign your rules AgentLink asks whether to keep them on; anything but yes turns them off, and a yes is remembered. Opened, accepted and rejected receipts work as before.
