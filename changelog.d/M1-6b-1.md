section: Added

- Large files can be uploaded to your own S3-compatible storage in parts (M1-6b part 1, S2, 07 §5.2): an interrupted or failed upload is always cancelled, so nothing half-uploaded is left in your bucket, and leftovers from a crash can be cleaned up. A storage safety net can also be set: a bucket rule that deletes AgentLink files after 30 days, added next to any rules you already have, never replacing them. Encrypting large files on the fly and using this for sending files follow in part 2.
