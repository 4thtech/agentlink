- Restore from a recovery kit, in the app and in the terminal (UX1113-R1, S2, D-147, 04 §2.3.3): the app finds kits in Downloads, Desktop and your home folder (or takes one you drop in), opens it with your recovery code, shows what comes back (counts and dates only) and restores your IDs without ever overwriting one. On a computer with an owner it asks for Touch ID or your master code first; on a new computer it ends with Touch ID and shows a new master code once. `agentlink restore [file] [--check]` does the same in your own terminal; `--check` only checks that a kit opens. The restore screens in the app come next (UX1113-R3).
