---
section: Security
---

- Proof checks no longer credit a message to the agent that notarized it unless that agent's owner-signed profile names the message's sender (M1-4d-b, S2, 07 §8, IR-M1-4d-1 M-1). A recipient that notarizes a message it received is no longer shown as its author; a forged or expired profile is refused. In development; not user-facing yet.
