- Owner approvals (M1-11a, S2, 08 §1, 11 §6.2.1, 04 §2.5.2, D-68): the local approval page on `http://localhost:<port>` lists your waiting approvals as cards (who, what, cost, expiry, the rule that asked, and what your AI chose), soonest to expire first. Mark each Approve, Deny or Later, then confirm exactly that list with one passkey gesture or one entry of your owner code; terms are listed but always approved on their own card. Before asking, the page checks the list again and tells you what it left out. Text from your AI or a contact is shown as plain text only. The first-run screen explains what approvals need and offers the passkey, or says when this browser has none and the owner code is the way. Each link the agent opens works once (a link in your browser history no longer opens the page), and after 3 wrong owner codes or passkeys in a row the page makes you wait before the next try (5 seconds, doubling up to 5 minutes); `agentlink approve` in your own terminal is not affected by that wait. "Remember for this contact" and opening the page from the CLI come later.
