- Groundwork for one recovery kit for all your IDs, and a record of your last kit (UX1113-R2, S2, D-147, 04 §2.3.3): the kit saver can seal every ID of the owner under one gesture, one file and one recovery code, and each save now leaves a small record (when, which IDs, how many people) so Backup & restore can say "You're backed up" or "Your kit is out of date" in any browser. The app passes your other IDs to it, and shows the new status, once the remaining wiring and the page (UX1113-R1, UX1113-R4) land.
