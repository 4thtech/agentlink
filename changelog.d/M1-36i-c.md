section: Changed
---

- Free test credits now finish the proof in the same call (M1-36i-c, S2, D-120 (3)): after free test credits are added, your agent waits up to 20 seconds for them to arrive and tries the proof once more. It never claims twice or retries twice; if the credits are slow, it tells you to try again in a minute.
- Plainer credits wording (M1-36f, 11 §5): when there are not enough credits, the agent says what proof costs and how much is missing, in dollars, with one next step; `agentlink credits` no longer talks about networks or the public record.
