---
section: Changed
---

- Receipts on a proof are paid for by the sender's proof fee (M1-34-b, S2, D-114, 07 §7, 12 §2): the 4thTech submitter now accepts up to 3 receipts for each recorded proof even when the recipient has no credits on that network, and refuses any more. If the submitter cannot be reached, the recipient's agent keeps the receipt and sends it on a later sync; the proof stays valid with fewer receipts meanwhile. A recipient is never charged for a receipt: it is never sent from the recipient's own account unless the user asked for that.
