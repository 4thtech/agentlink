### Added

- Fee quotes, credits and top-ups in the chain client, in development and not user-facing yet (M1-4b, S2, 07 §6.2, 12 §4, §5): the client quotes a proof or a registration from the fee vault itself and shows it in USD with the AgentLink fee and any app fee separate, reads the agent's prepaid credits on every supported chain, and prepares a direct top-up (an approval of exactly the amount, then the deposit). Only the stablecoin fee tokens are ever priced, shown or deposited.

### Changed

- 07 §6.1 and §6.2 describe the read client's URL-free view, the address-checked fetch requirement, and the quote, credit, deposit and credit-event rules (M1-4b; proposed, the owner confirms at merge). The technical docs' SDK packages page is updated, marked in development.

### Security

- No RPC endpoint address can be read from the chain client object any more, including through `extend`, a namespaced action, an `onError` callback or the transport; and the product path refuses to build a read client over a plain `fetch`: it needs the address-checked fetch that refuses private and loopback addresses at resolve time. This guards against wiring mistakes, not against a malicious caller in the same process (M1-4b, IR-M1-4a-2 R-1 to R-3, IR-M1-4b-1 L-1, I-7, 04 §4). Credit history ignores events from any address other than the configured fee vault and zero-amount deposits, sweeps and withdrawals (IR-M1-3b-1 I-2).
