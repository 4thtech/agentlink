- Proof of agreement through the tools (M1-9d-3, S2, 06 §2.2a, 04 §3.4.2; J8): `agentlink_propose_terms` with `notarize` sends a firm offer and records it, and `agentlink_respond_to_terms` `accept` with `notarize` records the acceptance (nothing is charged when the offer already has a proof). Each side's owner approves once, and that one approval covers the terms and the proof fee. With no credits, nothing is sent and the owner is not asked. The other side's one approval adds the Accepted receipt, and the exported proof verifies with the owner's signed approval. A recorded counter-offer or rejection is not available yet.
