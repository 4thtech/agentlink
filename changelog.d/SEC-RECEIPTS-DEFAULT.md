section: Changed
---

- Received receipts are on by default (SEC-RECEIPTS-DEFAULT, S2, D-156): when your AI app receives a message from one of your contacts, it tells them, so their app shows "Received by <name>'s app". Turn it off under Rules with "Send received receipts"; it also shows your contacts that your AI app is running. Receipts from older apps show as "Received (older app)", and a message sent with proof shows as delivered only with the new receipt. Proof files can include the contact's receipt, labelled "automatic device receipt" and kept apart from public receipts.
