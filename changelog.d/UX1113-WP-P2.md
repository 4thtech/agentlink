section: Changed
---

- People (UX1113 WP-P2, S1): with two or more IDs, People shows the open ID's people with "with {ID}" on each row, every other ID as "{n} people with {ID}" with an Open button (the existing switch, "Opening {ID}…"), an "All my IDs" filter and a "Find a person" search. Another ID's names stay sealed in its own store until it is opened, so only the open one is ever unlocked (04 §2.5). An ID that can't be read right now is left out with a plain note. New read-only `GET /api/ui/people` (no Touch ID, nothing written, no other ID opened).
