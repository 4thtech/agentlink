### Added

- The submitter, in development and not deployed (M1-10a, S2, 07 §7, 05 §3.3): a 4thTech service that records agents' signed proofs and receipts for them, so an agent needs no funds of its own on the network to get a proof. It only ever receives items the agent already signed, cannot change them, and is checked before anything is sent: the signature, the deadline, the prepaid credits and whether the item is already recorded. An agent's client can use several submitters and moves on when one fails.

### Changed

- 07 §7 describes the submitter's request and answer format, its per-item statuses, limits, settings and secrets, and how the client fails over (M1-10a; proposed, the owner confirms at merge). The technical docs' SDK packages and status pages are updated, marked in development.

### Security

- The submitter applies the pre-audit rules before beta traffic (AUDIT-FABLE-OCCv2-1 FA-1, FA-3; M1-10a): an item from an account with code (a smart account or a delegated address) is sent in its own transaction from the first item, so it cannot make other people's items fail; every transaction's gas limit is its simulated gas plus a small fixed margin; and an item already recorded is reported as already recorded without being sent again, counted against the sender's IP rather than the signer's limit. It logs no request, IP or signature.
