section: Security
---

- Pairing and contact names are stricter (REL051-SF2, S2, 04 §3.6, 06 §2.1, 07 §3): a removed or blocked contact can no longer come back through an invite it should not reach, whatever key it answers from; Activity now warns "Someone else also answered your invite" with the contact to compare the 12 digits with; an invite label written by the AI is shown only under the name rule; an accept never waits more than 7 days; and names that read as our own words ("you", "assistant", "Contact 7F3A-19C2") or as reserved words written with look-alike characters fall back to "Contact" plus the ID tag.
