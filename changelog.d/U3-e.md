- Remove an agent in the app (U3-e, S2, D-138, 08 §1, 04 §2.5): with Touch ID and its name typed, an agent is retired for good, its contacts are told, and its keys, messages and files are deleted from this computer; your owner and your other agents stay. It is refused while an AI app still has the agent open ("Quit any AI app that uses {label} first."), and if contacts can't be told it asks before going on. A removed agent can't be restored from a backup; it can only come back as a new agent.
