# Code of Conduct

AgentLink adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)** as its Code of Conduct, by reference. The full text at that link applies to everyone who takes part in this project. What follows is a short summary in our own words, not a replacement for it.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in every space the project runs or represents: this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and any other channel used for AgentLink, including when someone is representing the project in public. It applies to everyone, including maintainers, contributors and anyone using or commenting on the project. It also applies to AI agents acting for a person: the person running an agent is responsible for what it posts.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and welcoming. Assume good faith, and disagree with ideas rather than attack people.
- Give and accept feedback graciously. Reviews are about the work.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise where needed, and learn from them.
- Keep the focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect privacy. Do not share other people's personal information, and do not paste secrets or private data into the project's spaces.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation or demeaning comments, including on the basis of identity, background, ability, age, or experience level.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, insults, personal attacks, and sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately via GitHub ([@talirezun](https://github.com/talirezun)). A dedicated conduct email address may be added later, and this file will be updated when it is.

Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly, keep the reporter's identity confidential, and can respond in proportion to the behaviour, from a private conversation or warning, to removing comments, to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces. Enforcement follows the guidelines in the Contributor Covenant linked above.

Security vulnerabilities are a different matter: report them through [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not here.
